# Centipede-Snail-Fly Creature

> No name was given in play. This page title is descriptive, built from Adam's words in [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals|S04]] ("centipede slash snail slash fly"). [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] later calls it "the creepy crawler" ([[Session 07 — Omission of Truth|S07]]). Seth's "It's a hydra" (S04) is player speculation, not canon.

## Classification
- *(S04)* A monster of [Unknown/Ambiguous] type: red, part centipede, part snail, part fly (Adam, via [[Orin]]'s eyes) [01:54:10], [01:56:43].
- *(S05)* **"A natural pest of draconic beings"** ([[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]], Arcana 17, on a speared sample) [00:42:15]–[00:42:59].
- *(S05)* [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]], who built the dungeon, **has never seen it** [01:28:46], [01:40:00]–[01:40:41].

## Physical Description
- *(S04)* **Red**, many limbs and pincers; **bigger than a humanoid**; **engorged** from feeding (Orin, Perception 18) [01:56:41].
- *(S04)* Clawed limbs that can dig into a target and hold it.
- *(S04)* After [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] smashed its head, the head bubbled and **a new head jumped out, then another**; Adam: "Two separate bodies are slinging from this dead body" [02:28:14]–[02:28:53].
- *(S05)* The two new bodies are **separate and smaller** than the first (Tobias, Perception 19) [00:09:45]. Session-note label: "insectoids."
- *(S05)* Has **eyes** (Eliza's sling hit one in the eye; Bebo harvested an eyeball) and a **hide** large enough to cut a backpack-size piece from.

## Abilities Observed
- *(S04)* **Leap:** cleared the dragon's corpse in one jump to reach [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]].
- *(S04)* **Claw + grapple:** a critical claw on Bruin (8 piercing after a failed CON save); the claw stayed dug in and held her (grappled) until her STR save 25 [02:06:49]–[02:15:05].
- *(S04)* **Redirected cold:** twisted so part of [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]]'s Ray of Frost splashed into Bruin (2 cold) [02:10:02].
- *(S04)* **Mind resistant while raging:** saved against both Mind Sliver casts (INT save 15 each); "the magic can't get through its rage" [02:11:43], [02:14:09].
- *(S04)* **Regrowth / splitting:** two new heads emerged from the smashed one.
- *(S04)* Lost a limb to Bruin's greataxe; one limb frozen solid by Tobias's ice bolt.
- *(S05)* **Feeds on magic, but only on physical manifestations of magic**; it was feeding on the dragon (Bebo, Arcana 17).
- *(S05)* Surprise-attacked [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] on spawning (both missed); a later leap at Bruin (25) was turned by Silvery Barbs.
- *(S05)* **Burns:** both bodies ignited in Bebo's grease and screamed.
- *(S05)* No distinctive smell (its eye, held up for Bruin).

## Behavior
- *(S04)* Found **eating the corpse** of [[The Dungeon Dragon|the dragon]] behind the objective chest; Eliza heard the munching [01:53:06].
- *(S04)* Looked up, tilted its head at the approaching Bruin, then attacked. Fled behind the corpse when wounded.

## Threat Level
- *(S04)* Took at least 18 + 9 + 6 + 5 + 11 + 20 damage across two rounds and kept going in a new form. **Active at the end of S04.**
- *(S05)* **Dead.** Both phase-two bodies killed in two rounds with no party damage: Bruin decapitated one, Eliza's sling exploded the other's head. Harvested by Bebo (hide, eyeball). *(S07: the eyeball is being made into goggles of night.)*
