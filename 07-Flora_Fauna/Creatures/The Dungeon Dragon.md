# The Dungeon Dragon

> No name was given in play. Adam let the word slip mid-combat: "It barrels over the dragon and the like, I guess I called it a dragon already. Cat's out of the bag" ([[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals|S04]] [02:17:36]). Its nature beyond that, and why it was in the dungeon, are unconfirmed.

## Classification
- *(S04)* Called "the dragon" by the DM; [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]]: "That thing looks like a dragon" [02:26:18].
- *(S05)* **Dragon blood, of an unknown strand:** "some type of dragon blood," but "a strand of blood of dragon you have never ever heard of before" ([[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]], History 24) [00:44:27]–[00:44:34].
- *(S05)* Adam, as Tobias takes its heart: "you have a heart from the Creature of Dungeon Zero" [01:07:41]. *(Term ≤50%: label or STT unclear; confirm with Adam.)*
- *(S05)* [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]], who built the dungeon, **has never seen it** ("Neither the dragon lizard nor the insectoid") [01:40:00]–[01:40:41].
- *(S06, per the written recap)* Called **"the Cherry-Blossom looking Dragon from the dungeon"** and "the strange dragon-like creature." [[Delilah]] has heard of such things **only in fairy tales from her childhood** ("They aren't supposed to turn out to be real, right?"); [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]] found **a book with an obscure fairy tale** related to it (content not stated).
- *(S06)* **Its heart resists Identify:** it gives off magic, but Artie's powers are "currently unable to comprehend this object fully."

## Physical Description
- *(S04)* A **large lizard-shaped skeleton** with **bright purple / pinkish flesh and scales**, flower petals around it [01:46:56].
- *(S04)* Partly eaten down to the skeleton; part of the corpse was blown off by [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]]'s thrown [[Derek]] device [02:21:53].
- *(S05)* **Scales shift between deep purple, light pink and white, "kind of like a cherry blossom"**; cherry blossoms "are believed to bring good luck" (Bebo, Nature 21) [01:02:57].
- *(S05)* Ribs "the size of a long sword"; teeth; a tail bone; hide; a **heart from which magic emanates** [01:09:20].
- *(S05)* Its scale's scent is **overwhelming** to [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] ("The orange in your nose is what you smell right now") [01:44:01]. *Meaning not asserted.*

## Abilities Observed
- None (dead when found).

## Behavior
- None observed.
- *(S05)* At the top of round two its **half-skeleton lifted its head and fell**; the reverberation knocked Artie and Zarna prone; then it went completely still [00:29:55]–[00:30:13]. Cause not stated.
