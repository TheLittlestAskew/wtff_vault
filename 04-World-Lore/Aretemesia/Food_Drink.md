World Lore - Food and Drink

Meals often begin with a moment of acknowledgment—to the land, the harvest, or those who prepared it.

Waste is deeply frowned upon; preservation methods are highly refined.

Travelers are often offered food first, questions second—it’s seen as a way to “ground” someone in the land.

Each region exports signature ingredients that shape cuisine across the continent (thanks to the rail).
—----------------------------
🔥 **The Burn — Food of Endurance and Transformation**
Food in the Burn is about making something from almost nothing.
Flavors: Smoky, bitter, mineral-rich, with surprising depth
Cooking Style: Open flame, stone ovens, ember-burial cooking
Staples: Charred root vegetables, fire-resistant grains, ash-cured meats
🍴 Signature Dish: Emberbroth — a slow-cooked stew infused with smoked herbs and mineral salts, said to “restore what’s been burned out of you”
Drink: Cinder Tea — dark, slightly bitter, brewed from scorched leaves and served hot enough to sting
Nothing is wasted—burnt edges are valued, not trimmed away.
 Eating here feels like survival turned into craft.

🌿 **Rhus Valley — Food of Abundance and Celebration**
Here, food is joy, community, and generosity.
Flavors: Sweet, tangy, herbaceous, bright
Cooking Style: Roasting, baking, slow-simmering, communal feasts
Staples: Fruits, berries, grains, honey, fresh dairy
🍴Signature Dish: Sevenfold Harvest Platter — a rotating spread representing the valley’s seasonal bounty
Drink: Bloomwine — a lightly fermented fruit wine, often shared freely
Meals are long, shared, and often noisy.
 To eat alone here feels… almost unnatural.

🌊 **Liriope River Net — Food of Movement and Adaptation**
In the River Net, food is shaped by motion and immediacy.
Flavors: Fresh, briny, citrusy, lightly spiced
Cooking Style: Quick searing, steaming, pickling
Staples: Fish, river plants, algae, floating grains
🍴 Signature Dish: Current Wraps — handheld bundles of fish, herbs, and rice-like grains wrapped in aquatic leaves
Drink: Driftwater — lightly fermented river tonic, slightly effervescent
Meals are often eaten standing, walking, or aboard moving platforms.
 Food here is meant to travel with you.

🌸 **The Bloom — Food of Cultivation and Precision**
Cuisine in the Bloom is intentional, experimental, and beautiful.
Flavors: Floral, layered, sometimes unexpectedly complex
Cooking Style: Infusion, fermentation, controlled growth techniques
Staples: Edible flowers, rare herbs, cultivated fungi, nectar extracts
🍴 Signature Dish: Petal Array — a carefully arranged dish where each bite is meant to be eaten in a specific order
Drink: Nectar Elixirs — vibrant, sometimes magical drinks with subtle effects
Food is plated like art, and meals are often quiet, reflective experiences.
 You don’t just eat—you experience design.

🌫️ **Salvia Forest — Food of Healing and Awareness**
In Salvia, food is deeply tied to well-being and perception.
Flavors: Earthy, calming, herbal, subtly sweet
Cooking Style: Gentle preparation—steeping, slow drying, infusion
Staples: Medicinal herbs, mushrooms, forest fruits, roots
🍴 Signature Dish: Whisper Stew — a mild, restorative dish said to “settle the mind”
Drink: Dreamleaf Infusion — teas that can calm, focus, or even induce controlled visions
Meals are quiet, often shared in small groups or silence.
 Food here is less about fullness, more about balance.
