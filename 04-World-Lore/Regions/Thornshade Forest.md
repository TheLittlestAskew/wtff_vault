# Thornshade Forest

> Source: [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s backstory (pre-campaign). Vega's birthplace and home region.

## Overview
A forest of towering trees; the region where [[Vega Bloodroot]] was born and raised. Home to forest-dwelling monstrosities — one of which killed her mother [[Graza]]. Harbors a "rotted heart" from which aberrant creatures emerged during the [[Siege of Hollowbarrow|Siege of Hollowbarrow]].

## Sub-Locations
- [[Mossmere]] — Vega's home village.
- [[Hollowbarrow]] — site of the siege.

## Notable Features
Dense, towering tree cover; dangerous fauna (forest monstrosities); a corrupted "rotted heart."
