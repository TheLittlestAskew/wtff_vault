World Lore - Culture: Music, Art, Literature**

## 🔥 **[[The Burn]]** — Art of Memory and Survival
In the Burn, creation is an act of defiance.
+**Music** is percussive and raw—drums made from charred wood, rhythms that echo heartbeats and marching feet. Songs are often call-and-response, meant to be forgotten communally rather than written down.
+**Art** uses ash, scorched metal, and fractured glass. Murals are temporary, layered over older ones, creating a visual history of destruction and rebuilding.
+**Literature** is sparse but powerful—oral epics, survivor testimonies, and short, sharp writings that carry weight in few words.
Nothing here is made to last forever.
 Everything is made so it is not forgotten.

---

## 🌿 **[[Rhus Valley]]** — Art of Abundance and Community
In the Rhus Valley, creation is shared.
+**Music** is bright, melodic, and communal—string instruments, flutes, and harmonies that feel like conversation. Songs often change slightly each time they’re performed.
+**Art** is woven into daily life—quilts, pottery, dyed fabrics, and carved wood. Beauty is practical, and practicality is beautiful.
+**Literature** is generational—stories passed down, added to, and adapted. Many tales have no single author, only a lineage.
Ownership of art is less important than participation in it.
 To create alone is seen as… incomplete.

---

## 🌊 **[[Liriope River Net]]** — Art of Motion and Impermanence
Here, creation flows.
+**Music** is fluid and improvisational—wind instruments, water drums, and layered rhythms that mimic currents. Performances shift depending on the environment and audience.
+**Art** is temporary by design—floating sculptures, dyed currents, patterns made on water that disperse within hours.
+**Literature** is nonlinear—stories told in fragments, out of order, or from multiple perspectives. Meaning is something you navigate, not receive.
To fix something in place is to misunderstand it.
 Art here is meant to be experienced… and then lost.

---

## 🌸 **[[The Bloom]]** — Art of Growth and Intention
In the Bloom, creation is cultivated.
+**Music** is intricate and layered—structured compositions that evolve over time, sometimes incorporating living elements like plant resonance or environmental sound.
+**Art** is living—sculptures grown from vines, flowers trained into shapes, entire installations that bloom, wither, and return.
+**Literature** is philosophical and deliberate—essays, poetry, and treatises exploring beauty, balance, and the ethics of creation.
Nothing is accidental.
 Even chaos is… carefully guided.

## 🌫️ **[[Salvia Forest]]** — Art of Perception and Truth

In Salvia, creation is discovery.
- +**Music** is quiet, ambient, and often barely perceptible—wind chimes, breath tones, and subtle harmonics that seem to appear rather than be played.
- +**Art** is hidden or shifting—carvings that only appear in certain light, patterns that change depending on where you stand.
- +**Literature** is introspective—journals, riddles, fragmented poetry. Many works are meant for a single reader, not an audience.

You do not simply observe art here.
 You are changed by it.

🌼 **[[The Flower Court]]'s** Relationship to Art
The Court does not just support the arts—it relies on them.
Major decisions are sometimes preceded by artistic interpretation of a problem.
Each Petal surrounds themselves with creators who reflect their philosophy.
--[The Pistil] is said to favor works that reveal connections between regions—pieces that show the whole, not the parts.
