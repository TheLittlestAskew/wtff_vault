The Flower Court

The Flower Court is the living political and spiritual heart of the continent—a governing body shaped not by conquest or bloodline, but by alignment with the land itself. Each region is represented by a Petal, a leader chosen (or recognized) for embodying the essence of their domain. Together, the Petals form the Court, a delicate balance of philosophies: destruction, growth, protection, change, and understanding.
At the center of it all sits the Pistil—not a ruler in the traditional sense, but the axis upon which the Court turns.
The Court convenes in a sacred location known as the Sepal Sanctum, a circular, ever-blooming chamber where each Petal’s presence causes the environment to subtly shift—ash drifting in one quadrant, water pooling in another, vines creeping quietly along the stone. No Petal holds more formal power than another… but all defer, in some way, to the Pistil.
Because the Pistil does not command.
The Pistil binds.
—---
**Structure & Function of the Court**
+Five Petals, One Voice Each
 Every Petal speaks for their region’s needs, values, and concerns. Their authority is absolute within their domain—but in the Court, they must negotiate, persuade, and sometimes concede.
+Consensus Over Command
 Decisions are rarely made by majority alone. The Court strives for consensus, believing imbalance in agreement reflects imbalance in the land itself. When consensus fails, tension rises—and the Pistil may be forced to act.
+Seasonal Convergence
 The Court meets formally at each seasonal turning (four times a year), though emergencies can call them together sooner. Each meeting subtly reshapes the Sanctum—winter might frost the stone, spring might cause blooms to overtake the walls.
+Envoys & Roots
 Each Petal maintains a network of agents—called Roots—who act as diplomats, spies, messengers, and problem-solvers across regions. These Roots are often where adventurers come in.
