### **The Burn** — The Ashen Stand of Kael Vire
Long before Zelkova City stood as it does now, the Burn was a raging, unending wildfire said to have been fed by something deep beneath the earth. The legend tells of Kael Vire, a lone warden who refused to abandon the land when others fled. With only a handful of survivors, Kael led a desperate stand at the edge of the advancing inferno, carving firebreaks by hand and holding a defensive line for seven days and nights. On the final night, Kael descended into the heart of the blaze itself—and the fire broke. When it cleared, the land was blackened, but the flames were gone. Kael was never seen again, but in Zelkova City, it is said that when embers drift against the wind, the Warden is still walking.
 
### **Rhus Valley** — The Covenant of Seven Harvests
In an age when the Rhus Valley’s abundance first drew envy from beyond its borders, a coalition of warbands marched to claim its fertile lands. Rather than meet them in open war, a group known as the Seven Stewards—farmers, herbalists, and community leaders—crafted a different defense.
 Over seven seasons, they reshaped the valley itself: redirecting rivers, cultivating dense, thorned groves, and planting crops that sustained their people but choked invading supply lines. When the warbands finally arrived, they found a land that could not be taken—only survived within. Defeated not by swords but by the valley’s will, they withdrew. To this day, Rhusatatiam honors the Covenant as proof that patience and unity can outlast even the sharpest blade.

### **Liriope River Net** — The Fleet of the Thousand Paths
When the tides once rose unnaturally high and threatened to swallow the entire Liriope River Net, the waterways became a maze of death—currents shifting, channels collapsing, and entire settlements drifting toward the sea. In response, a loose alliance of navigators, fishers, and wanderers formed what would become known as the Fleet of the Thousand Paths. Led by a figure forgotten only as The First Navigator, they mapped the ever-changing waters in real time, guiding people to safety along routes that existed only for moments at a time. For weeks, they ferried thousands to higher ground, often returning through impossible currents to save more. When the waters finally calmed, the Fleet dissolved as quickly as it had formed—but every navigator since claims their routes were not entirely their own.

### **The Bloom** — The Quiet War of Lethira Vale
At the height of the Bloom’s early expansion, its unchecked growth began to overtake nearby regions, smothering ecosystems and threatening balance across the land. The story tells of Lethira Vale, a botanist and mage who saw what others would not admit—that the Bloom itself had become dangerous. Rather than destroy it, she waged a quiet war of correction, cultivating countergrowths, introducing stabilizing species, and reshaping the spread from within. For years, no one knew the scale of her work; fields that might have consumed entire regions instead settled into harmony. By the time her efforts were understood, Lethira had vanished into the very wilds she helped temper. In Galium Major, her legacy remains a guiding truth: not all growth is good—and wisdom lies in knowing when to prune.

### **Salvia Forest** — The Veilwalkers’ Passage
There was a time when the Salvia Forest was said to swallow those who entered, its paths shifting endlessly and its whispers driving travelers mad. Then came a small, quiet group known as the Veilwalkers, seekers who believed the forest was not hostile, but misunderstood. Led by a figure called Eryn of the Listening Step, they spent years moving slowly through the woods, learning its rhythms, mapping not its paths but its intentions. 
They emerged not with a map, but with a way of moving—rituals, pauses, and patterns that allowed safe passage. From that day forward, Willow Haven was founded, not by conquering the forest, but by understanding it. Even now, those who rush through the Salvia Forest are said to be lost… while those who listen may find themselves guided.
