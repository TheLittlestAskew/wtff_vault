Law on the Continent
#### “[The Accord of Balance]”
Across the entire continent, law is built on a shared foundation known as:
The Accord of Balance

> ***“No action may take more from the whole than it returns.”***

This is not written in one place.

It is taught, interpreted, and enforced through culture, leadership, and the Flower Court.

There is no single legal code.
*Instead, there are:*
- Shared truths (universal law)
- Regional expressions (local rule)
- Court-level intervention (when balance breaks)



#### [The Role of the Petals]
Each Petal is the final authority in their region—but they are not rulers in the traditional sense.

They:
- Interpret the Accord of Balance
- Intervene when systems fail
- Act as judges in extreme cases

But they are expected to embody their region’s philosophy, not override it.



### 🌼 **Universal Laws** (Agreed by All Regions)
These are the closest thing to “hard law” on the continent—violating these will bring attention from beyond just your region.

#### 1. The Right of Return
You cannot deny the cycle of death.
No permanent entombment
No binding of souls
No destruction that prevents return
Breaking this is one of the gravest offenses.
#### 2. The Sanctity of the Line (The Train)
The Petaline Line is neutral ground.
Violence aboard is forbidden
Trade cannot be unlawfully seized
Passage must remain open
If this is broken, the Court responds immediately.
#### 3. The Balance of Taking
You cannot extract from land or people without giving back.
Overharvesting
Exploitation
Hoarding critical resources
This is less about punishment and more about restoration enforced by others.
#### 4. The Autonomy of Regions
Each region governs itself.
No Petal can impose rule over another region directly.
This prevents war… mostly.
##### 🔥 **The Burn — Law of Consequence**
Law here is direct and unforgiving.
If you harm the community, you are expected to restore what was lost
If restoration is impossible, you may be exiled or marked
Trials are short, often decided by respected survivors
There is little patience for abstraction.
“What did you break—and how will you fix it?”

##### 🌿 **[[Rhus Valley]] — Law of Community**
Law is decided collectively.
Disputes are brought before gatherings
Outcomes focus on repairing relationships
Repeat offenders may be cut off socially, which is devastating here
No one is above the community… but no one is abandoned easily.

##### 🌊 **[[Liriope River Net]] — Law of Flow**
Law is flexible, situational, and adaptive.
Rules change depending on context (water levels, movement, need)
Leaders and navigators make judgment calls in the moment
Contracts are often verbal and binding through trust
Justice is about keeping things moving—not getting stuck in blame.

##### 🌸 **[[The Bloom]] — Law of Design**
Law is structured, deliberate, and philosophical.
Rules are written, studied, and refined
Violations are analyzed as failures of balance
Punishment often involves guided correction or reassignment of purpose
This is the closest thing to a “formal legal system” on the continent.

##### 🌫️ **[[Salvia Forest]] — Law of Truth**
Law here is… unsettling.
Guilt is determined through understanding, not evidence
Confession carries more weight than accusation
Some disputes are resolved through ritual, memory, or altered perception
You cannot lie easily here.
But you may not like what the truth reveals.
