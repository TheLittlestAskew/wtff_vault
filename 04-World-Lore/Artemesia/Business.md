**The Petals**
The Petals are the governors of Artemesia’s major regions.
Each Petal oversees a territory shaped heavily by geography, culture, resources, and local economic identity. While officially loyal to the Pistil, many Petals compete for political leverage through trade influence, military strength, or control of critical resources.

*Their authority includes:*
Regional taxation
Resource management
Trade enforcement
Local military oversight
Regulation of guilds and commerce
—--
**Wealth and Currency**
In Artemesia, wealth is measured in more than coin.
True power often exists in five forms.

**Currency**
Coin remains the most common form of exchange.
*Used for:*
Daily trade
Wages
Taxes
Transportation
Contracts

Though many citizens casually refer to all currency simply as “Seeds,” the system separates money into Seeds, Stems, Sprouts, and Buds depending on value.

Within criminal markets and smuggling circles, illegal or counterfeit currency is commonly referred to as “P-Side Coins,” short for pesticides. The term originated as slang among black market traders who joked that dirty money spread through commerce like rot through a garden.
Despite the name, P-Side Coins are not always counterfeit. The term is often used broadly for:
-smuggler currency
-stolen coinage
-unregistered trade bars
-forged rail tender
-black market payment tokens
-hidden guild currencies

**Common Illegal Goods**
-Counterfeit rail seals
-Forbidden alchemy
-Monster organs
-Unregistered magic
-Memory extracts
-Stolen cargo

-Forged travel permits

**Currency System**
Currency is standardized across the continent through minting overseen by the Pistil.

**Denominations
Seeds** — copper currency used for daily purchases and common trade
**Stems** — silver denominations commonly used in regional commerce and wages
**Sprouts** — gold denominations used by merchants, nobles, and large trade contracts
**Buds** — rare platinum denominations typically reserved for immense wealth, political transactions, and major guild exchanges
Though standardized currency exists, some regions continue to rely partially on barter, favors, or weighted trade systems.

\*\*+Commerce of Connection+\*\*

Not all business in Artemesia revolves around physical goods.

Some industries exist purely around emotion, memory, and experience.



\*\*Memory Houses\*\*

Specialists capable of preserving, extracting, or recreating memories.

Clients may relive:

Lost love

Childhood experiences

Traumatic events

Victories

Final conversations



\*\*Story Brokers\*\*

Travelers who trade rumors, oral histories, political secrets, and legends.



\*\*Grief Gardens\*\*

Sacred spaces where flowers are cultivated to preserve emotional memory tied to the dead.



\*\*Echo Parlors\*\*

Businesses where individuals experience curated emotional states or re-lived sensations.



\*\*Final Philosophy of Commerce\*\*

Commerce in Artemesia is transformative.

Trade does not simply move objects.

It moves:

\-ideas

\-culture

\-language

\-faith

\-memory

\-ambition

\-fear

\-identity



A farmer may become worldly after a single journey.

A merchant may gain more influence than a noble.

A traveler may arrive home transformed.



In Artemesia, commerce is not separate from civilization.

Commerce is civilization.
