# 🗓️ The Calendar of Artemesia

*Also called the **Petal Cycle.** Time flows like petals: each cycle a season, each season a story.*

---

## ⏳ Core Time Units

| Unit | Equals |
|---|---|
| 1 Year | 12 Bloommonths |
| 1 Bloommonth | 30 Days |
| 1 Week | 6 Petaldays |
| 1 Day | 24 Hours (120 Heartbeats) |

---

## 🍃 The Five Seasons

The Flower Year moves through five seasonal phases, each with its own theme:

| Season | Theme |
|---|---|
| Spring | Renewal |
| Summer | Growth |
| Late Bloom | Reflection |
| Autumn | Harvest |
| Winter | Rest |

---

## 🌸 The Twelve Bloommonths

Each Bloommonth is ruled by a flower and carries the energy of its season. Listed in calendar order (1 through 12):

| # | Bloommonth | Flower Title | Season |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Liliora | Blossom of Beginnings | Spring (Renewal) |
| 2 | Ayvelle | Petal of Awakening | Spring (Renewal) |
| 3 | Veriden | Bloom of Warmth | Summer (Growth) |
| 4 | Solenia | Flower of Abundance | Summer (Growth) |
| 5 | Florentis | Bloom of Elation | Late Bloom (Reflection) |
| 6 | Noctura | Petal of Reflection | Late Bloom (Reflection) |
| 7 | Serevia | Bloom of Change | Autumn (Harvest) |
| 8 | Arundel | Petal of Harvest | Autumn (Harvest) |
| 9 | Emberlys | Bloom of Remembrance | Autumn (Harvest) |
| 10 | Frostine | Petal of Stillness | Winter (Rest) |
| 11 | Lunaria | Bloom of Dreams | Winter (Rest) |
| 12 | Silvora | Petal of Rest | Winter (Rest) |

> **Note:** The image places months 1 to 12 around a wheel, with seasons spanning arcs. Spring covers the top, moving clockwise through Summer, Late Bloom, Autumn, and Winter. The exact season boundaries for each month are inferred from the wheel's color bands and the season arc labels; adjust if your campaign defines them differently.

---

## 🌼 The Week: 6 Petaldays

Each week has six days, each named after a part of a flower:

1. **Stamen Day**
2. **Petal Day**
3. **Leaf Day**
4. **Stem Day**
5. **Root Day**
6. **Seeding Day**

---

## 🌙 The Three Moons of Artemesia

Artemesia has three moons, each governing a different rhythm of life:

| Moon | Epithet | Form | Governs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lumora** | The Guide | Largest and brightest | Purpose, protection, clarity |
| **Velora** | The Watcher | The crescent moon | Intuition, secrets, memory |
| **Petalis** | The Gentle | Smallest, often hidden | Dreams, emotions, fate |

---

## 🎉 The Four Major Festivals

| Festival | Tied To | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **The First Bloom** | Liliora | A festival of new beginnings, wishes, and planting |
| **High Bloom** | Solenia | A celebration of life, light, and community |
| **Harvest Accord** | Arundel | A time of gratitude, feasts, and honoring ancestors |
| **Night of Reflection** | Noctura | Lanterns are released for lost loved ones and hopes |

## ❤️ Timekeeping: The Heartbeat

Days are measured in **Heartbeats.**

- 1 Day = 24 Hours = 120 Heartbeats
- (This works out to 1 Heartbeat = 12 minutes.)
