🗓️ **The Calendar of Artemesia**🗓️

*Also called the **Petal Cycle** or **the Flower Year**. "Time flows like petals, each cycle a season, each season a story."*

**Start of Adventure Date:** the evening of the **1st of Veriden**, the **75th year post-Heroes** (Adam, [[Session 01 — Winds in the East|S01]] [00:19:18]: "You are in the first of Veriden").

### 🌸 The History of the Artemesian Calendar

The **Artemesian Calendar**, often called the **Petal Cycle**, was created after the Ashfall Wars that ended the Age of Heroes.

Before its creation, each region tracked time differently. The people of the Burn counted years by major fires and rebuilding efforts. The River Net measured seasons by flood patterns. Rhus Valley followed harvests. The Bloom maintained elaborate astronomical records, while Salvia Forest often ignored formal calendars entirely, preferring memory and observation.

The lack of a shared system created confusion as trade expanded and the newly formed Flower Court sought to unite the continent without forcing cultural conformity. The solution came from an unlikely collaboration between scholars from all five regions, led by the Pistil at the time, **Canctitious Weidenmyre**, who believed that time itself should reflect the values of Artemesia.

Rather than dividing the year by kings, wars, or numbers alone, they chose to model it after the life of a flower.

A flower begins in possibility, grows through abundance, yields its gifts, rests, and eventually awakens once more. The scholars saw this cycle mirrored not only in nature but in civilizations, communities, and individual lives.

Each month was named not for rulers or gods, but for moments in a living cycle. Festivals were tied to gratitude, remembrance, growth, and renewal rather than conquest or victory. Even the symbols chosen for each month were flowers native to one or more regions of Artemesia.

Over time, the calendar became more than a way to mark days. *It became a philosophy.*

Children are taught that no season is superior to another. Growth requires rest. Beauty requires change. Harvest requires patience. Endings make beginnings possible.

Today, nearly every home, train station, tavern, and Court hall keeps a Petal Calendar. While regional traditions still differ, the Petal Cycle remains one of the few things shared by all people of Artemesia—a quiet reminder that balance is not found in a single moment, but in the entire cycle.

As an old Flower Court inscription reads:
> *"Count not the days that pass beneath your feet. Count the blooms, the harvests, the rests, and the awakenings. Time is not something we spend. It is something we cultivate."* 🌸

## 🌼 The Flower Year

*From the calendar plate (image at the bottom of this page).*

The people of Artemesia track time not by numbers alone, but by the blooming and resting of the world around them. One year is **12 Bloommonths**, each ruled by a flower and the energy of the season.

### ⏳ Key Information

| Unit | Equals |
|---|---|
| 1 Year | 12 Bloommonths |
| 1 Bloommonth | 30 Days |
| 1 Week | 6 Petaldays |
| 1 Day | 24 Hours (120 Heartbeats) |

*Derived, not on the plate:* 1 year = 360 days; 1 Bloommonth = 5 weeks exactly; 1 Heartbeat = 12 minutes.

### 🍃 The Five Seasons

| Season | Theme | Bloommonths |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | Renewal | 1 Liliora, 2 Ayvelle |
| Summer | Growth | 3 Veriden, 4 Solenia |
| Late Bloom | Reflection | 5 Florentis, 6 Noctura |
| Autumn | Harvest | 7 Serevia, 8 Arundel, 9 Emberlys |
| Winter | Rest | 10 Frostine, 11 Lunaria, 12 Silvora |

> ⚠️ The plate shows the seasons as arcs around the wheel, not as a list, so which months fall in which season is **read off the picture**. Two are borderline: **Ayvelle (2)** sits where the Spring and Summer arcs meet, and **Serevia (7)** sits where the Late Bloom and Autumn arcs meet. Confirm with [[Adam]] if it ever matters.

### 🗓️ The Week: 6 Petaldays

Each week has six days, each named after a part of a flower:

1. **Stamen Day**
2. **Petal Day**
3. **Leaf Day**
4. **Stem Day**
5. **Root Day**
6. **Seeding Day**

### 🌙 The Moons of Artemesia

Artemesia has **3 moons**, each governing a different rhythm of life.

| Moon | Epithet | Description | Represents |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lumora** | The Guide | The largest and brightest | Purpose, protection and clarity |
| **Velora** | The Watcher | The crescent moon | Intuition, secrets and memory |
| **Petalis** | The Gentle | The smallest, often hidden | Dreams, emotions and fate |

### 🎉 Major Festivals

| Festival | Bloommonth | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **The First Bloom** | Liliora (1) | A festival of new beginnings, wishes and planting. |
| **High Bloom** | Solenia (4) | A celebration of life, light and community. |
| **Harvest Accord** | Arundel (8) | A time of gratitude, feasts and honoring ancestors. |
| **Night of Reflection** | Noctura (6) | Lanterns are released for lost loved ones and hopes. |

### ❤️ Timekeeping: The Heartbeat

Days are measured in **Heartbeats**. 1 Day = 24 Hours = 120 Heartbeats.
