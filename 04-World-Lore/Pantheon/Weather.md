# DHORUBA
aka **THE STORM**

**![[06-Media/Pantheon/Weather 1.png|360]]**

# DETAILS

The Goddess of the Weather has been around since the dawn of time. Throughout history, she has observed feats of greatness, both light and dark.

The core beliefs towards which she has always gravitated are following one’s passions, being devoted to those around them, and both seeking out and following truth; the truth of the universe and personal truths held within. The storm cannot be contained. It is powerful. It is brutal. It can bring destruction, and it can bring growth.

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS:


|The biggest taboo for a follower of Dhoruba is falsity. This includes lying to oneself in order to chase a false dream. Being true to oneself is the only path towards a relationship with the Goddess. Until someone sheds all of their walls and insecurities, bearing themselves before the storm, they cannot be in true communion with Dhoruba.
