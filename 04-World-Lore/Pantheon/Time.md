# APPEARANCE


a large warforged with four arms. Glowing ember eyes… he stares off into the distance. Seeing everything at every time. 

His heart ticks/ beats like a clock… keeping the world in rhythm and time. 

The magic organ emits a bright amber glow and is rumored to be from his creator

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS:

The Unalterable Past - The past is fixed and sacred. A core tenet is to forbid or severely punish tampering with, or lying about, the past, as it is the foundation of the present. 

The Responsibility of the Moment - While time is eternal, a specific moment is fragile and fleeting. Followers must seize the right opportunity, act decisively, and respect the "right time" for action. 
  
Prayers and offerings are to be made at midnight. The end of one day and the beginning of the new day.
