# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS


### Witness, Do Not Turn Away

You will not look away from the suffering you helped create.

Followers must:

- Tend the wounded (enemy or ally)
    
- Remain after battle until the last breath is accounted for
    
- Bear witness to the consequences of violence
    

---

### End What You Begin

You do not abandon the aftermath.

This includes:

- Rebuilding what you broke
    
- Burying the dead
    
- Facing those affected
    

---

### Protect Those Who Cannot Fight

War is not permission—it is responsibility.

Followers must:

- Defend civilians first
    
- Intervene when the defenseless are targeted
    

---

### Carry Memory

Forgetting is a second death.

Many followers:

- Keep tokens from battles (names, cloth, ash)
    
- Record histories of conflict truthfully—not glorified
    

---

### Do Not Seek War—But Do Not Fear It

War is not to be desired. But neither is it to be ignored when it comes.

This keeps them grounded:

- Not pacifists
    
- Not warmongers
    

---

### No One Is Beneath Burial

Enemy or ally, all are given rest.

Mass graves without rites are a grave offense.

---

### You Are Not the Judge—Only the Witness

You do not decide who deserved it.

This is HUGE philosophically:

- They observe, act, endure
    
- But do not claim moral authority over death
