# DETAILS

Her main companion is a large shadow figure with a crow beak. Some say she is able to hear whispers through crows.

 All must treat the creatures with the utmost respect. Some may provide offering to keep in her favor. 

They say that to have disrespect a crow is to disrespect Siobhan and she shall reign vengeance.

# APPEARANCE

Long black hair, slightly wavy, down to her waist. Tan skin and black eyes. Red/black bird feathers poking out of her hair. Earthy colors. She holds herself high and has a strong and alluring/ intimidating presence.

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS

- Everyone must have equal opportunities to learn.

- Laziness and compliance is heavily frowned upon and seen as the worst insult.

- All questions may be answered to those who seek but they must be willing to accept what they find.

- Help must always be offered to those in need for the favor to be paid back by giving to someone else. It is through kindness and generosity that life lessons may be passed from person to person.

- One must not trust blindly and must always have a dose of skepticism.

- Precise details of current events must be accurately accounted for and scribed without bias to allow future generations to learn from the past and make a better future. (It will be an important part to have followers whose sole job is to explore and observe current events first hand to then relay it to scribes at each sanctuary. All sanctuaries are connected and they are able to travel from place to place with magic. You step inside a sanctuary and it feels like you travel through time. It is both old in the way they use candles and it looks like an 18th century library but they have technology and magic that goes beyond anything of that day and age.)

- All actions have consequences. Be warned
