# DETAILS


Vaelor teaches that power is the truest force in creation, but power comes in many forms: Strength of body, strength of will, wealth, influence, intelligence, courage, leadership, and the ability to endure suffering. (He despises those who inherit power, but do not deserve it*) 

Worship Style: Temples resemble courts, fortresses, arenas, mountain halls. Worship involves oaths sworn publicly, tests of endurance, competitive duels, acts of leadership and charity through strength, breaking chains as symbolic rites.

Power how I think of it: As a divine concept it can mean many things: Raw Strength, Political Control, Inner Discipline, Influence, Domination, Ambition, Authority, Corruption. (This is how I'm imagining you are describing the God of “Power” as). In my opinion - Power is Neutral, neither purely evil nor purely good. Power itself is neutral, it just depends who wields it. Therefore he is generally a Neutral God.


Relationships (general population): (Any of these may be in the campaign):

3. Merchants building empires
    
4. Ambitious nobles
    
5. Maybe Monks mastering discipline
    
6. Revolutionaries seeking to overthrow weak rulers (NOTE: Tyrants and heroes can both worship him)

# APPEARANCE

Appears as a towering humanoid figure of polished bronze or obsidian skin, depending on the viewers fears or ambitions. Eyes glow white. He wears a fractured crown that floats slightly above his head as well as a simple dark red and gold robe (think like Greek times). Probably muscular, but regal, not like monstrous. Voice sounds like many rulers speaking at once with a deep bass physical feeling. Carries no weapon - his hands alone imply power. When angered, gravity bends around him and weaker beings struggle to stand

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS


1. Become stronger each day - stagnation is failure
    
2. Rule yourself before ruling others
    
3. Protect what is yours, or prove you deserve to lose it
    
4. Take responsibility equal to your power
    
5. Respect strength wherever it is found
    

**Taboos:**

1. Begging for what you can earn
    
2. Abusing the weak purely for amusement
    
3. Squandering talent
    
4. Hiding behind titles without merit
    
5. Surrendering from FEAR alone.
