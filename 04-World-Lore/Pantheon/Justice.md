# ERYNDOR
"The Thorned Judge"

**![[06-Media/Pantheon/Justice 1.png|379]]**


# SYMBOL

A closed eye wrapped in a ring of thorns with a single flower blooming in the middle. (Pic attached). The closed eye represents impartiality—not blindness, but reflection before judgment. The thorns symbolize that truth is painful and justice is rarely gentle. The flower at the center represents memory—something delicate, but enduring

# APPEARANCE

A towering figure with skin resembling pale bark veined with faint gold light. His eyes are always closed until judgment is passed. From his shoulders grow flowering vines that shift species constantly, each bloom representing a memory or truth being weighed. When angered or delivering verdicts, the vines tighten, and the blossoms wilt into thorns

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS

1. “Nothing Done Is Ever Lost.”
    

Followers must believe that all actions leave a mark—if not in law, then in memory, spirit, or the world itself.

2. “Judge Only After Witness.”
    

Clerics and Paladins of Eryndor are forbidden from passing judgment without understanding context, intent, and consequence.

3. “Truth Must Be Preserved, Not Twisted.”
    

Altering records, memories, or testimony for convenience is a grave sin—even if it would produce a “better” outcome.

4. “Justice Is a Garden, Not a Blade.”
    

Punishment must aim toward balance or restoration when possible—not mere retribution. 

  

**Taboos:**

1. Destroying evidence of the past, including magical or natural memory (objects and artifacts are considered memories of the past).
    

2. Forcing confessions through coercion or torture—truth gained this way is considered “poisoned”.
    

3. Passing judgment in haste or anger.
