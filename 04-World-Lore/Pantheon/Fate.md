# **MANEKI**

**![[06-Media/Pantheon/Fate 1.png|320]]**

# DETAILS

It is also said that Maneki's shadows move with a dancer's grace, seemingly independently. Their purpose is to weed outcomes that weren't meant to be. To cross Maneki's shadows, it is said, is not to die, but to simply be unmade


---

# SYMBOL

a pristine white housecat, with living shadows that walk in her wake. It is said that all housecats have a fragment of Maneki's power. They can both see someone's fate, and Maneki can see through all of their eyes. Thus, when a cat stares intently, they are looking not at you, but at what you will become and might have become had your life gone differently.

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS:

Followers of Maneki believe several things:

1. Under Maneki's gaze, what is meant to be will be.
    
2. Beneath Maneki's shadows, what was not meant to be is culled.
    
3. All cats hold a fragment of Maneki's essence, and must be treated as sacred
    
4. To defy fate is to tempt Maneki's shadows
