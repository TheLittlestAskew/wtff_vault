# **REVERIE**

*She Who Forgets*
Goddess of Love, Memory, Longing, and the Eras of the Heart


**Reverie is not saccharine. She is not soft edges and flowers without thorns. She is romantic but unflinching -- she will show you the beautiful and the devastating truth of a love, and she will not look away from either.

**She is fiercely protective of those who love without being loved back. They are her particular devotion.**

**She will walk the earth quietly, bending circumstances so that lovers find each other sooner, easier, faster. A missed train that becomes a meeting. A storm that keeps two people in the same room. She meddles gently and without announcement.**

**She has a temper. Those who betray love -- any love, not only romance -- will find her appearing in the face of the person they hurt. She does not punish cleanly or quickly. She lingers. She meddles. She makes them sit inside what they have done until remorse becomes real. She keeps receipts. She always has.**

### Reverie governs all forms of love -- not just romance:
- First loves and found family
- The love that survives loss
- The love that should not be, but is anyway
- The love of a story so good you grieve when it ends
- The terrifying love that makes you brave
- The eras of love across a single lifetime -- first love, mature love, grief, chosen family, self love, the love that quietly changes everything
- She also holds domain over memory as an act of devotion. To forget someone is, in her theology, a form of worship.

# TEMPLE

**Reverie has no churches.**
She has never asked for walls or roofs or doors. 
Instead, she asks that her clergy construct open circles of thirteen great stones called ***Standing Vigils*** -- each one oriented to ***The Watcher***, built with careful attention to the sky visible from that exact location. No two Standing Vigils are identical because no two skies are the same.


---

# CLERGY

Her clergy do not live at any single site. They travel between the Standing Vigils, moving from circle to circle the way stars move across the sky -- always in motion, always returning.

When her clergy gather at a Standing Vigil they stargaze, speak the small prayers aloud, sing love songs, and look for The Watcher. Finding his heart the night after a storm and holding it in your gaze long enough to feel something is considered an answered prayer.

The teacup does not fear the storm. It was made to hold it.
This saying is carved into the base of every Standing Vigil's threshold stone.


---

# SYMBOLS

## THE GOLDEN THREAD

She is said to tie fates together with threads mortals cannot see. The threads are visible only in the moments immediately following a life event that draws two people closer to each other -- a flash, gone before it can be questioned. Her clergy believe the threads were always there. The moments simply make them briefly legible.

## THE STORM IN A TEACUP

Her most personal symbol, and it means something different depending on who is reading it. Her clergy do not consider that a contradiction. They consider it the nature of love itself.

She carries the storms of the goddess who made her possible, kept small enough to fit inside something you could hold in two hands. Wild weather, made intimate. A daughter's love for a mother she did not choose and did not expect.

The mortal vessel. The teacup is an ordinary life. The storm inside it is what Reverie places in every heart that loves -- vast and turbulent and real, living inside something fragile that was not built to contain it, and does anyway.

What remains. When love ends the storm does not. It stays inside the cup, smaller now, quieter, but present. Contained. Never gone.

# APPEARANCE

Reverie's true form is a woman with long black dreads, medium dark skin that glows faintly in starlight, and violet eyes. Her eyes never change. In every form, in every face, the eyes remain violet -- and those who know what to look for will always find her.

She does not usually appear to mortals as herself. She appears as whoever they have loved most.

The eyes give her away. They always do.
