# **VELMORA**
The Mourning Star

**![[06-Media/Pantheon/Death 1.png|274]]**

# SYMBOL

A blue tear suspended within a silver crescent representing sorrow, memory, and peaceful passage into death

**![[06-Media/Pantheon/Death 2.png]]


# APPEARANCE

Velmora appears as a tall, graceful goddess. She has glowing blue eyes, and long pale blue hair that flows like moonlight. She wears midnight blue and silver robes adorned with teardrop jewels, and star like ornaments. Wears hood over her face out of mourning.

She uses soft silver bells that ring only for the dead. Souls follow the sound to their final place.

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS

## Tenets:

- Remember the dead and speak their names
    
- Comfort the dying so no soul passes alone
    
- Honor grief as sacred, never shameful
    
- Treat bodies, graves, and funeral rites with dignity
    
- Allow souls to pass freely into rest
    
- Preserve memories, histories, and final words
    

## Taboos:

- Binding, trapping, or enslaving souls
    
- Necromancy that denies peaceful rest
    
- Desecrating graves or bodies
    
- Mocking grief or forcing others to “move on”
    
- Erasing someone’s name, memory, or legacy
    
- Seeking immortality through stolen life or stolen souls
