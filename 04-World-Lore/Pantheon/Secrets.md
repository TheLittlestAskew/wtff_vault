# **SWORYN**
the Veiled Messenger

**![[06-Media/Pantheon/Secrets 1.png|339]]**

**Sworyn is loved by all the gods and fully trusted by none. He moves between domains because every divine force has secrets, hidden motives, sacred truths, and dangerous revelations. He may preserve a secret, reveal it, delay it, or guide it to the right hands.**

# DETAILS

In stories, he is the god who: 

- shelters forbidden truths until the proper hour 

- exposes betrayal when silence has become corruption 

- protects confession 

- carries the last words of the dying +reveals paths that had remained unseen 

- awakens talents and truths hidden within a soul 

- works quietly behind the actions of both gods and mortals


---

# SYMBOL

Sworyn’s symbol is made of two mirrored hooked glyphs divided by a central veil-line, visually resembling two opposing question-mark forms, with the left side reversed. It represents the duality of secrets: one side conceals, one side reveals

# APPEARANCE

Sworyn appears as a shadowed divine figure more than a fully physical being. He is draped in deep midnight-blue and black robes with subtle gold filigree, and his hood is fully integrated into the robes so that he seems half-clothed, half-formed from darkness itself. His face is almost entirely hidden beneath veil and shadow, with only a single golden eye visible, glowing like a small flame in the dark. His body fades into drifting shadow-fog and smoke, as though he is not made of flesh in the same way other gods are. He carries sealed letters, keys, lockets, and charms that represent entrusted truths, hidden paths, and unspoken promises. His presence is elegant, ominous, quiet, and divine, like a being who exists just behind every confession, every betrayal, and every secret too heavy to bear alone.**


---

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS

Followers of Sworyn are taught that secrecy is a burden, a shield, and sometimes a weapon. They are expected to understand the weight of hidden things.

- Keep what is entrusted to you, unless silence protects cruelty. +Respect private grief, private love, and private prayer. 

- Reveal truth only when its hour has come. 

- Do not expose another person’s hidden wounds for pride, entertainment, or gain. 

- Guard the hidden gifts of others until they are ready to claim them. 

- Understand that secrecy is not the same as deceit. 

- Know that some truths must remain veiled, while others become poisonous if buried too long. 

  

Sworyn forbids: 

- betraying sacred confidences for personal benefit 

- spreading false secrets or rumors +exposing pain simply to shame, entertain, or control 

- +wearing secrecy lightly and breaking it carelessly 

- hiding truths that must be spoken to prevent grave harm 

- using hidden knowledge only to dominate others
