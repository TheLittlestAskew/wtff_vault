# APPEARANCE

Mera always appears as someone who belongs in whatever space she is in. Whether sitting in an empty chair as if she was always meant to be there She could be found randomly folding laundry that no one remembers leaving out Just softly present in places where she is needed 

She appears differently to each of her followers, but always with a familiar look in her eyes. 

Her form to them appears to be that of soft edges and unremarkable features. (Like a childhood memory that you can’t quite remember but always gives you that warm feeling.) 

The constant feature that her followers always describe is her deep brown eyes that always seem relieved to see you. 

Mera’s true form is simple. Long lavender hair tied back with a grey ribbon with deep brown eyes. The clothes that she wears is a patch work dress that seems to be covered with soot as if from a fireplace she had been hard at work cleaning.

# COMMANDMENTS/TENETS


- Always leave a place better than you found it 

- Protect your family (Does not always mean blood)

- Offer shelter to weary travelers (Even enemies who ask for shelter are temporarily protected by law, UNLESS they choose to betray that protection.) 

- Always Keep a light on whether a fireplace or candle at night

**Taboos:** 

- Entering someone’s home without invitation is dangerous 

- Manipulation in the form of hospitality 

- Withhold healing or care to prove a point 

- Leaving your home or community without just cause (Random punishments) 

- Food never quite satisfies or heals completely 

- You don't get a complete short or long rest
