# Hollowbarrow

> Source: [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s backstory (pre-campaign). Site of the Siege of Hollowbarrow.

## Description
A site within the [[Thornshade Forest]], scene of the **Siege of Hollowbarrow**.

## Notable Features
Near the "rotted heart" of the forest, from which a horde of aberrant creatures spilled during the siege.

## Inhabitants

## History
During the Siege of Hollowbarrow, aberrant creatures poured from the rotted heart of the forest. [[Vega Bloodroot]], [[Varro]], and the [[Mossmere]] militia fought day and night and won, but at great cost. The battle is the source of Vega's recurring nightmares.
