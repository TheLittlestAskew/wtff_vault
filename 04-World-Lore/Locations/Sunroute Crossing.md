## Description
A **rail-stop village** and the site of **Group K**'s first [[The Uprooters|Uprooter]] test dungeon. Group K arrived aboard [[The Verdant Blossom]] (green train) from [[Rhusatatiam]] just after midday in [[Session 03 — Raise a Glass to Provisional Trust]]. The station sign reads: *"Where endings took root and new beginnings grew."* The town is reached by crossing a bridge; it is **community-funded** (workers well-paid, tipping uncommon — [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]], History nat 20), with **festival season approaching** ("the first of vent was yesterday," per [[Delilah]]). Broken-down **ruins on a hill** conceal the entrance to Aiph's dungeon.

- Region: [Unknown/Ambiguous]

## Notable Features
- **[[X Marks the Spot Inn]]** — the central inn/tavern/tower where Group K lodged the night before the dungeon.
- **The test dungeon** — a **five-layer** dungeon personally curated by [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]], entered via a **trap door** in a nondescript ruin. ~25–30 ft ladder down to a pitch-black empty stone room. Objective: reach the bottom layer, retrieve a chest's contents, return them to Aiph. "Some of it is dangerous, some simulates danger."
- Morning market on the way up to the ruins.

## The Test Dungeon: Layers (S04)
*From [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] (07/26/2026). The DM framed the riddles as fitting "the narrative that she wants to test all kinds of different versions of the party" [01:30:40]. Nature-themed throughout (Druidic script, walking trees, a flower potion, a "very druidic" door).*

| Layer | Description | Notable Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The pillar chamber | Rectangular stone chamber; the ladder is gone once the party lands ("just the wall of a cave"). Six pillars, four carrying riddles in **Druidic**. | Answers **walls, arrows, cloud, silence**; a lone answer triggers the walls falling and unseen projectiles (6 piercing, no marks left). Each trap is the answer to the next riddle. Conjuration magic in the walls. The final answer silences everyone and opens an **illusory hole** in the floor. |
| 2. The tree chamber | "Almost identically sized rectangular chamber" of dirt and grass with four large trees; ~10 ft drop onto soft earth. | **Noise triggers lashing vines.** A pulsing orb (abjuration) behind each tree makes it walk; ~60 ft soft-earth circle at the center. The trees merge into a **walkway down**; returning an orb to the roots gives a d4 of healing or temporary HP. |
| 3. The flower cauldron | "Much smaller chamber, still circular"; six flower beds (six rows of four) around a flaming cauldron embossed **"four blooms, five petals."** | Parchment letter grid on a corner table (answer: gold, black, purple, gold, white). Six wooden cups; the potion teleports the drinker and grants advantage on Wisdom saves for an hour. [[Tobias Wolfe\|Tobias]] painted a symbol on the wall. |
| 4. The door of hands | A dark area with a light ahead; steps up to a massive door sculpted with **hands**. | No handle or lock. [[Eliza Duskbloom\|Eliza]] pulled the hands off (they decayed to dust), leaving a plain wooden door that opened only once all six pushed. |
| 5. The domed chamber | "Massive circular" dome, walls covered in multicolored moss. | The objective **chest** (conjuration magic; unopened at the end of S04); the corpse of [[The Dungeon Dragon\|a dragon]] being eaten by the [[Centipede-Snail-Fly Creature]]. S04 ends mid-fight here. **S05:** fight won; chest opened (600 gp, two gems, a jar of spheres, a black dagger); dragon harvested; a **door outline on the far wall lit up soft green** when Bebo spotted it, and stepping through returned the party to the **ruins at the surface**. |
