# Mossmere

> Source: [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s backstory (pre-campaign). Vega's home village.

## Description
A small but tightly-knit village within the [[Thornshade Forest]]. Vega's childhood home; the village watched over her and [[Varro]] like extended family.

## Notable Features
Has a local militia, which [[Vega Bloodroot]] joined on coming of age.
