# X Marks the Spot Inn

> ⚠️ **First-seen (≤60% confidence).** Named in [[Session 03 — Raise a Glass to Provisional Trust]]; confirm spelling with [[Adam]].

## Description
The central **inn / tavern / tower** at [[Sunroute Crossing|Sunroot Crossing]] where **Group K** lodges the night before descending into [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]]'s test dungeon. Reached by crossing a bridge into town. A **loud, warm** open floor plan with a **band playing dead-center**, a **bar along all four walls**, and a **spiral staircase** up to the guest rooms. Flashing a mahogany [[The Uprooters|Uprooter]] token covers the stay.

## Notable Features
- Band in the middle; bars on every wall; recurring "cat aggro" gag (a white-mittened cat leaping onto the bars).
- Guest rooms upstairs (one key per PC).
