# The Verdant Blossom

> ⚠️ **First-seen (≤60% confidence).** Named in [[Session 03 — Raise a Glass to Provisional Trust]]; confirm spelling with [[Adam]].

![[Verdant_Blossom_Interior.png]]

## Description
The green **"lounge" luxury train** that carries **Group K** (the WtFF party) from [[Rhusatatiam]] to [[Sunroute Crossing|Sunroot Crossing]] on their first [[The Uprooters|Uprooter]] mission. Its interior is the color of a hothouse — plants, soft chairs, and glass. The ride is roughly **two hours** (arriving just after midday). Flashing a mahogany Uprooter token covers costs aboard.

## Notable Features
Cars named by [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]] during her walkthrough:
- **Lounge car** (car 2) — soft chairs and books; where [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] tended her axe and spoke privately with Aiph.
- **Dining car** (car 3) — bar and food service; an elderly human server sold [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] "two of everything" in sweets.
- **Conservatory / garden car** (car 4) — window-mounted growing beds with communal produce; growth is subtly magically assisted ([[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]], Nature 13). [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]] harvested carrots here; the pod met [[Constance]].
- **Parlor / library** — ordinary take-a-book-leave-a-book fiction ([[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]], Investigation 22).
- **Guest cabins 1 & 2.**
- **Observation car** — an all-window viewing sphere at the rear.
- **Staff & storage car** — crates (supplies, locked personal effects, a chest of confiscated weapons; [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]], Investigation 24) and a workbench where [[Derek]] tinkers.

DM art: `06-Media/Verdant_Blossom_Interior.png`.

## Inhabitants
- **[[Constance]]** — elven magic-shop owner from [[The Burn]]; a passenger met in the conservatory car.
- **[[Derek]]** — manic tinkerer from [[The Bloom]]; train staff at the staff & storage workbench.
- **Unnamed elderly human dining-car server.**
