# Mouth that Feeds

## Description

The capital city of the [[Liriope River Net]]. Less a single city and more a living network — built atop stilts, platforms, and interwoven canals at the convergence of countless waterways and the sea. Homes drift, markets relocate, and entire districts subtly shift with the currents. Fishing fleets, trade vessels, and flat-bottomed ferries create a ceaseless hum of activity; water gardens and floating farms sustain the population.

The people here are adaptable, sharp-eyed, and deeply attuned to the rhythms of tide and current. To outsiders the city feels chaotic; to those who belong, it is perfectly balanced — survival depends on flow, not control.

*(World document content, confirmed by Adam OOC at [[Session 02 — I Can Feel It In My Bones]]; marked with a crown icon on the [[Shmeetz]] world map.)*

## History

When tides once rose unnaturally high, threatening the entire [[Liriope River Net]], a loose alliance of navigators, fishers, and wanderers formed the **Fleet of the Thousand Paths**. Led by a figure known only as the First Navigator, they mapped ever-changing waters in real time, guiding thousands to safety along routes that existed only for moments. The Fleet dissolved when the waters calmed; every navigator since claims their routes were not entirely their own.
