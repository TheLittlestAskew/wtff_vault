Capital: [Willow Haven]

**Details:**
Hidden within the golden-green canopy of the Salvia Forest, Willow Haven feels like a secret the world has not fully uncovered. The city is built in harmony with towering willow and salvia trees, with homes suspended among branches or nestled at their roots. Sunlight filters through leaves in shifting patterns, and the air carries a constant whisper of wind through foliage. Known for its healers, herbalists, and quiet mystics, Willow Haven serves as a place of restoration—many travel here seeking remedies not only for the body, but for the spirit.
Paths are intentionally winding, and the city reveals itself slowly, as if testing the intentions of those who enter. 
Those who stay long enough begin to understand: Willow Haven does not simply shelter its people—it listens to them.

Salvia Forest — The Veilwalkers’ Passage
There was a time when the Salvia Forest was said to swallow those who entered, its paths shifting endlessly and its whispers driving travelers mad. Then came a small, quiet group known as the Veilwalkers, seekers who believed the forest was not hostile, but misunderstood. Led by a figure called ]Eryn of the Listening Step], they spent years moving slowly through the woods, learning its rhythms, mapping not its paths but its intentions. 
They emerged not with a map, but with a way of moving—rituals, pauses, and patterns that allowed safe passage. From that day forward, Willow Haven was founded, not by conquering the forest, but by understanding it. Even now, those who rush through the Salvia Forest are said to be lost… while those who listen may find themselves guided.
