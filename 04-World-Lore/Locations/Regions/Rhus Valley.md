**Rhus Valley** — [The Covenant of Seven Harvests]
Capital: [[Rhusatatiam]] 

**Details:** 
Rhusatatiam sprawls gracefully across the fertile plains and winding waterways of the Rhus Valley, a city that feels more grown than built. Terraced gardens, berry orchards, and flowering groves weave between low, sun-warmed buildings, creating a vibrant tapestry of color and life. The air is thick with the scent of ripening fruit and crushed leaves, and seasonal festivals shape the rhythm of daily life. Trade flows easily here, with river barges and road caravans converging on bustling market plazas. Rhusatatiam is known for its deep-rooted traditions and communal spirit—decisions are often made in open forums beneath sprawling canopy trees, where elders and youth alike gather. 
It is a place of abundance, but also of careful stewardship, where every harvest is seen as both gift and responsibility. 

In an age when the Rhus Valley’s abundance first drew envy from beyond its borders, a coalition of warbands marched to claim its fertile lands. Rather than meet them in open war, a group known as [the Seven Stewards]—farmers, herbalists, and community leaders—crafted a different defense.
 Over seven seasons, they reshaped the valley itself: redirecting rivers, cultivating dense, thorned groves, and planting crops that sustained their people but choked invading supply lines. When the warbands finally arrived, they found a land that could not be taken—only survived within. Defeated not by swords but by the valley’s will, they withdrew. To this day, [Rhusatatiam] honors [the Covenant] as proof that patience and unity can outlast even the sharpest blade.
