The Burn — The Ashen Stand of Kael Vire
Capital: [Zelkova City] 

**Details:** 
The people of Zelkova carry a quiet resilience; they honor what was lost, but refuse to be defined by it. At the city’s heart stands [the Ember Archive], where stories of [[The Burn]] are preserved—not as warnings, but as promises that nothing, not even fire, is final. 

Zelkova City rises from ash and memory, a capital forged in the aftermath of devastation. Built among blackened ridgelines and cooled lava flows, the city is a striking blend of scorched ruin and stubborn regrowth. Pale, fire-hardened zelkova trees—twisted yet alive—line the streets, their roots breaking through cracked obsidian roads. Architecture here favors dark stone and heat-resistant glass, with structures designed to withstand both flame and time. 

Long before [Zelkova City] stood as it does now, the Burn was a raging, unending wildfire said to have been fed by something deep beneath the earth. The legend tells of [Kael Vire], a lone warden who refused to abandon the land when others fled. With only a handful of survivors, Kael led a desperate stand at the edge of the advancing inferno, carving firebreaks by hand and holding a defensive line for seven days and nights. On the final night, Kael descended into the heart of the blaze itself—and the fire broke. When it cleared, the land was blackened, but the flames were gone. Kael was never seen again, but in Zelkova City, it is said that when embers drift against the wind, the Warden is still walking.
