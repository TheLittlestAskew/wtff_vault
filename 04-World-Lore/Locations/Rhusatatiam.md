# Rhusatatiam

## Description
The **capital city of the [[Rhus Valley]]** — the first city named in play. It sprawls gracefully across the fertile plains and waterways of the valley, "more grown than built": terraced gardens, berry orchards, and flowering groves between low, sun-warmed buildings. Market roads converge beneath hanging lantern vines and flowing terraces; the air carries ripening fruit and crushed leaves. (See the [[Rhus Valley]] region page for the fuller portrait.)

## Notable Features
- *(S07)* **"A city of overgrowth"** (Alec): flowers, vines and leaves cascading down the buildings, brick walls hidden by foliage. To [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] it "feels weird" and "doesn't feel right" (neither forest nor garden). Not asserted as linked to [[The Overgrowth]].
- Home of the **[[Hearthread Hall]]** and its never-extinguished **Common Fire**.
- Known for deep-rooted communal traditions; decisions made in open forums beneath canopy trees.

## Inhabitants
Valley folk: farmers, herbalists, traders, and community elders. During [[Session 01 — Winds in the East]] the city drew candidates from across the continent for [[The Uprooters]]' recruitment.

## History
It is the **75th year post-Heroes**, the first of [[The Petal Cycle|Veriden]], with a **Bloom Festival** approaching at the time of the session. (Regional history: see [[Rhus Valley]] — the Covenant of Seven Harvests.)
