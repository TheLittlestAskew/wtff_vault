🌿 The Rootbound Circle
(Healers / Cycle Keepers)
A decentralized order devoted to maintaining [the Rite of Returning] and the health of the land. They travel freely between regions, ensuring that death, decay, and regrowth remain in balance.
They are often called when something feels wrong—blighted soil, lingering spirits, unnatural growth.
Vibe: Calm, grounded, quietly respected everywhere
**Goal:** Protect the natural cycle
