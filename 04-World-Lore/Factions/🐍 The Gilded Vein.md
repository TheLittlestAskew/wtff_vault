🐍 **The Gilded Vein** 
(Smugglers / Balance Breakers)
The Gilded Vein operates in the spaces between regions—using [the Petaline Line], hidden river routes, and forgotten paths to move goods that shouldn’t move. They don’t see themselves as criminals.
They see themselves as correcting imbalance.
If one region hoards resources, they redistribute them. If something is restricted, they make it available.
If the Court says “no”… they ask "why not?"
Vibe: Charismatic, rebellious, morally gray
Specialty: Moving restricted goods between regions
**Belief:**
 “If the world is meant to be balanced… why does it need to be controlled?”
