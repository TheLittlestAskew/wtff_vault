(Rail Authority / Logistics Power)
The Ironroot Consortium maintains, operates, and quietly controls much of [the Petaline Line]. 
Publicly, they are engineers, conductors, and schedulers. In reality, they are one of the most powerful organizations on the continent—because they decide what moves, when, and how.
They claim neutrality, bound by the Sanctity of the Line, but influence flows through them like current. Entire regions depend on their reliability, and even the Petals tread carefully around them.
Vibe: Professional, quiet power, logistical dominance
**Goal:** Keep the Line running—at any cost
