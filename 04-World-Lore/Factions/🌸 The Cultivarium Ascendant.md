🌸 **The Cultivarium Ascendant** 
(Botanists / Expansionists)
Based largely in the Bloom, this faction believes the continent is meant to grow beyond its current balance. They study, cultivate, and sometimes push ecosystems further than they should go.
They are not villains—just very comfortable taking risks others won’t.
Vibe: Elegant, intellectual, slightly unsettling
**Goal:** Advance growth and evolution
