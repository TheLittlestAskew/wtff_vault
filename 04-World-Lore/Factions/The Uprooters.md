# The Uprooters

## Overview
The **Uprooters** are the organization at the center of the campaign's launch. In [[Session 01 — Winds in the East]] their two recruiters ran an interview gauntlet at the [[Hearthread Hall]] in [[Rhusatatiam]] to assemble new members from a crowd of candidates. Each candidate was asked to introduce themselves and answer two core questions — *"Why the Uprooters?"* and *"How do you define good and evil?"* — plus an idiosyncratic follow-up. Their specific mandate and methods were not spelled out this session; recruitment, the changing world, and the broader balance of Artemesia are the threads in play. *(Purpose beyond recruitment: [Unknown/Ambiguous] as of S01.)*

## Leadership
Two recruiters ran the selection, each ultimately taking their own group:
- **[[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]]** — chaotic, tinkering alchemist-presenter; voiced by [[Adam]] (WtFF DM). Took **Group K** (the WtFF party).
- **[[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]]** — a druid with an ever-present protective animal companion; voiced by [[Alec]] (WtFR DM). Took **Group J** (the WtFR party). *(Crossover reference — WtFR card, not expanded here.)*

Aiph and Mags share a fraught, intimate, and lethal history ("I love you. But I will fucking kill you if you get in my way"; "Nobody's a hero. You and I know the last hero to ever exist"). They are positioned as a central mystery, not mere quest-givers.

## Relationship to Party
The six WtFF PCs are now Aiph's provisional recruits (Group K). Aiph specifically wanted [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] — partly for her shared "scent" sense of the changing world.

## Open Mysteries
- **The unnamed "he":** Both Aiph and Mags have referenced an unnamed male figure — "I would, by the way, do what he did. And that's why it needs to stop" (Aiph, S02); "would you do what he did if…" (Mags to Aiph, S02). This person appears central to why the Uprooters exist and why neither Aiph nor Mags want the Age of Heroes to return. Identity [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- **Groups A–I:** Aiph stated during the S02 orientation that groups A through I are "unaccounted for" — either gone or came back and "didn't last much past coming back." Fate unknown.
- **"He's getting stronger" (S07):** Mags: "That only means he's getting stronger"; Aiph: "I want to kill him." Whether this is the same "he" as S02 is [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- **The recruiters have lost control (S07):** Aiph: "We were so afraid after what happened last time that we waited too long... We are not in control anymore." Mags: "As soon as they have any reason to believe that you and I are not in control, the whole house falls." What happened "last time" is not stated.
- **Mirror secrecy (S07):** each recruiter asked her group to keep its dungeon's events from the other group.
- **"Our business our business" (S08):** Aiph asked Group K to keep their business their own; all six agreed (Bebo on condition it may speak if lives are at stake, for either group). Per Aiph, Mags will threaten to throw Group J out of the Uprooters if they talk; Aiph: "I will not extend such a threat."
- **The pivot (S08):** Aiph no longer knows "what's going on in these dungeons"; she directs Group K to [[The Burn]], "the really only remaining dungeon that I know of, not created by either Mags nor I," then "wandering Artemesia looking." Something "similar happened to" Group J in its dungeon (per Aiph; not recorded here).
- **Common goal, different methods (S08):** Aiph and Mags "have a common goal" but go about it "very, very differently." Adam (above-table DM framing): whatever is in the crater "in the center" is "ruining Artemesia." Aiph: "We, all of us now are uprooters."
- **"Orion would be pissed" (S08):** Mags, asked by Aiph to fire [[Dolm]] from the Uprooters: "I can't. I'm stuck. Orion would be pissed." Referent unknown (F12).
