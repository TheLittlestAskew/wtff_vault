## Character POV Journal
### Isla "Bruin" Kaplan — In-Character Journal Entry

**The Hall That Never Lets Its Fire Die**

I have never been so far from the water and felt it so loudly.

The hall sat at the center of the city like a heart with too many chambers — timber wrapped in living ivy, a fire in the middle that they say has never once gone out. Strangers everywhere. Farmers, players of instruments, people who write things down. I kept to the edges. I always keep to the edges. Nobody hears my feet when I don't want them to, and tonight I wanted to be a draft under a door, nothing more.

But the air was wrong. It has been wrong for a long while now, this thing I came so far to chase — the smell that rides on water that no longer runs the way it should. I thought I'd left it behind me on the road. I hadn't. It was here too, in a tavern packed with people who couldn't smell it at all.

Two of them were running this gauntlet up on the stage. One drew a circle in chalk and the noise of the whole world fell away inside it — cold, careful, the kind of person who measures you before you've finished sitting down. The other was a storm in a body: a bag spilling odd little things, a pinch of sand that caught fire in her fingers and changed color and changed again, a laugh sharp enough to cut. They called the rest up one by one. A scholar with golden eyes. A girl who hands out flowers. A painted boy with a fox on his shoulder. A turtle-child folding paper animals that breathed for a moment and then went still. A great gentle giant who broke a chair and then mended it, apologizing the whole time.

When my name was called I looked for a sign before I sat. She gave me one. And then she asked how far home was, and something in me decided to be honest. I told her the water doesn't run like it used to. That there's a scent on the air, and most people don't know it's there, and I need to know what it is and why it's reaching my home.

She went still. *I smell like that scent,* she said — searching my face for a reaction. So I took the bear down off my head and let her see the rest of my face, and I told her the truth I'd been carrying since the road: *I know. I can smell it from here.*

For just a breath the whole table changed. Whatever they'd planned, I'd stepped off the edge of it. The cold one and the storm had a quarrel after I left — quiet, but I felt the heat of it on my neck. Before I went she told me the only thing that has stayed with me all night: *trust your nose.* Then she told me to go away, so I did. I perched by the door. When the room got loud and warm and people started pressing in to learn each other's names, I slipped outside, where I could breathe and listen.

I don't know what I am to these people yet. When the choosing came, I felt the night tip over into something, and I missed it — too busy walking back through the door to read the look that passed between the two of them. But my name was on the storm's list. Whatever this is, she wants me in it. She wanted me *because* of the scent, I think.

I came to find out what is wrong with the world. I think I just agreed to walk straight into it.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary
The two campaigns launched together in a single 14-person call. After out-of-character logistics (recording consent, art-sharing etiquette, and an acknowledgement of [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]]'s player's birthday), [[Alec]] delivered the campaign's framing monologue: **Artemesia is a world deliberately built *after* heroes.** The Old World's age of singular greatness (the **First Blooming**) collapsed when one grieving hero — the **Hollow King** — turned their power against death itself, igniting the **Ashfall Wars** that scarred the continent (creating **The Burn**). In the ashes, survivors dismantled the cult of the hero and built **balance**: the regions ([[The Burn]], [[Rhus Valley]], [[Liriope River Net]], [[04-World-Lore/Locations/Regions/The Bloom]], [[Salvia Forest]]), [[The Flower Court]] led by a connector titled the **Pistil**, and the unifying railway, the **Petal Line** — *"A hero stands alone. A railway requires everyone."* Now the crater "grows stronger," **Bug Island stirs**, and rumors say the Pistil has begun acting alone.

[[Adam]] then set the scene at the **[[Hearthread Hall]]**, a sprawling, generations-old tavern in **[[Rhusatatiam]]**, capital of the [[Rhus Valley]], famous for its never-extinguished central hearth, the **Common Fire**. It is the **75th year post-Heroes**, the first of [[The Petal Cycle|Veriden]], with a **Bloom Festival** approaching. On the second floor's small stage, two recruiters for **[[The Uprooters]]** — the druid [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] (with an ever-present protective companion) and the chaotic, tinkering [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]] — began **interviewing candidates** to join the organization.

The bulk of the session was a **gauntlet of one-on-one interviews**, each candidate describing themselves and answering two core questions — *"Why the Uprooters?"* and *"How do you define good and evil?"* — plus an idiosyncratic follow-up. Mags drew a **chalk circle** that created a bubble of silence at her table. Candidates seen: [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]] (seeking why magic is misbehaving), [[Lute]] (who answers in her late mother's aphorisms), [[Nyx]] (an Aasimar paladin of a sun/justice deity), [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]] (a sentient construct who "would like to learn… and to survive"), [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]] (a timid druid both recruiters strangely felt they *knew*), [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] (drawn by a flyer and a love of history), [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]], [[Auriron]] (a fire genasi who burns what his god [[Purification & Cleansing|Mortheon]] deems evil), [[Dolm]] (a gentle Goliath told by his mentors **Tilo** and **Brindle** that it was his "turn to bloom"), and [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] (a naive painter with the fox [[Nero]]). Several throwaway candidates were dismissed, fled, or fought (Bram Hollowbarrel, Marnie Willowstep — who left crying, Tessil Goldpetal & Pella Bramblewine — who brawled and were ejected, Tovin Redfoot & Garrek Timberlane — absent/"cowards," Aldin Forgehill, Elaria Thorne).

Two beats broke the rehearsed pattern. **Bruin** told Mags she could smell "a scent on the air" that signals the changing world — and Mags, unsettled, replied that **Mags herself carries that scent**; Bruin removed her bear pelt and confirmed *"I can smell it from here."* This triggered a private, charged argument between the recruiters; Mags later warned Aiph that **if he picked Bruin, that would be "the only reason she leaves this tavern alive."** Separately, **Eliza's** interview left both recruiters convinced they *knew* her despite never having met (Mags silently signaled Aiph: *"you will pick Eliza"*).

During open mingling, [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]] danced badly but joyfully and tried to draw [[Nyx]] in; [[Auriron]] studied Nyx's deity symbol (recognizing it as religious but failing to name the god); [[Lute]] heard a voice **in her own head** and talked back to it, alarming [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]]; [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] tracked down food and sketched the room. Finally, three floors up, **Mags and Aiph** revealed a fraught, intimate history — debating whether *any* of these idealists could matter in a world that no longer believes in heroes ("Nobody's a hero. You and I know the last hero to ever exist"), professing love and mutual lethal threat ("I love you. But I will fucking kill you if you get in my way"), and a kiss — before agreeing to **each take a group**. Mags announced **Group J** (her WtFR party: [[Lute]], [[Nyx]], [[Rowan]], [[Kidu]], [[Auriron]], [[Dolm]]) and Aiph announced **Group K** (the WtFF party: [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]], [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]], [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]], [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]], [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]], [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]]). The session ended at the split, before the groups' separate endeavors began; Session 2 will reopen in the tavern. **No combat occurred.**

### Themes & Emotional Beats
- **"World post-heroes" / collective over the individual** — the campaign's thesis: *"A hero stands alone. A railway requires everyone."* The very act of assembling a team enacts the theme.
- **Grief as the engine of catastrophe** — the Hollow King's loss as the original sin of the Old World.
- **Outsiders seeking belonging** — Bruin (keeps to edges), Tobias (an "odd duck," wants to "find home"), Eliza (timid, unused to people), Kidu (used to being overlooked) all answer from the margins.
- **Love entangled with violence** — Mags & Aiph: *"I love you. But I will fucking kill you if you get in my way."*
- **The unrecognized sign** — Bruin's scent-sense marks her as an early warning the world can't read; Mags carrying that scent implies the recruiters are closer to the mystery than they admit.
- **Naïveté vs. knowing** — Tobias innocently perceives the recruiters' charged intimacy he "has no idea what he's looking at."
