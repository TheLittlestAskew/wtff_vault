# Session 06 — The Art of Staying Cool
> **Date:** 08/23/2026 *(inferred, see Archivist Notes)* · **Campaign:** [[Artemesia|Where the Flowers Forget]] · **Session type:** Regular WtFF session (Group K, DM [[Adam]])

There is no transcript, so there are no quotes, rolls, initiative or POV journal for S06. Adam's spoken recap at the start of [[Session 07 — Omission of Truth|Session 07]] confirms the title.

## Recap

[[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] flees from the tavern in search of a body of water to calm down. He finds a small pond not far from the town and sits on the edge. As he sets his legs in the water, he notices not his own reflection… but that of someone dear to him. He speaks aloud to those from his past how he hates them, how he loves them, how he misses them. As he wrestles with his pain and hatred, the reflection dissolves back to one of himself.

[[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]], [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]], and [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]] find Tobias (after leaving [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] to guard the frozen [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|BeBo]] in the tavern) and are able to talk him down from the edge. Tobias laments how he doesn’t really know how the ice happens, but it always seems to occur at moments of heightened emotion. Artie agrees to help teach Tobias some magic and how to possibly control it better. Eliza remarks that the fact that things didn’t go “according to plan” is probably a good thing for them as what lies ahead won’t be “planned” either.

After returning to Bruin and BeBo (Bruin has apparently attempted to pour fire whisky on BeBo hoping this may thaw them out) Tobias & Artie go find [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]] as Tobias wants to apologize for freezing the patrons of the tavern. Aiph mentions that Tobias’s powers aren’t a burden nor a liability. They are an asset. If he would only stop running from it, stop trying to control the power and simply let it flow through him he may find a power he did not know he had, one that the [[The Uprooters|Uprooters]] may need one day.

BeBo thaws out while Zarna converses with their favorite server, [[Delilah]]. She tells her about the strange dragon-like creature they encountered to which Delilah mentions she’s heard of such things but only in fairy tales from her childhood. “They aren’t supposed to turn out to be real, right?”

Back on the train overnight towards their original meeting place, Bruin rests while Artie leads BeBo & Zarna to the staff car to meet once again with [[Derek]], the artificer from [[The Bloom]]. He is overjoyed that Artie was able to use the explosive he gave him. Zarna, upon hearing Derek may have even more explosives, offers the group’s services as “Explosive Field-Testers” working title Derek gifts Zarna explosive ball-berings, Artie explosive ink with an activator quill, and BeBo a small light sphere which explodes with “healing dust.”

Eliza settles into the Observatory car to journal while Tobias converses once again with Aiph. He confides with her about how he was treated in his home town. How the people treated him… poorly for who he was and asks Aiph if she and [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] ever were looked down upon for loving one another. Aiph tells Tobias that, “there will always be those who look down upon others for something or other. It is merely a projection of their own lack and their own pains.” Tobias says he will never again set foot in his home town even though there is someone there he loves very deeply and misses. Aiph strikes a deal, “there is also a place which I never hope to return to one day. Perhaps we can help one another. You can go to my place for me and I to your place for you.”

Artie finds a book which has an obscure fairy tale related to the Cherry-Blossom looking Dragon from the dungeon. He then attempts to use “Identify” on the heart itself. While the heart of the dragon does give off magic, his powers are currently unable to comprehend this object fully.

A series of messages are exchanged between Aiph and Mags as Group K’s train gets a head start back to [[Rhusatatiam]], back to the tavern, back to the Commonfire where this journey began…
