## Character POV Journal
### Isla "Bruin" Kaplan — In-Character Journal Entry

**Back at the fire, and the city that smells wrong**

We came back to where it started. The same tavern, the same fire in the middle of it that nobody lets die. It isn't morning and it isn't evening. Afternoon, warm, loud.

The machine is moving again. It was frozen solid the last time I really looked, and now it's fussing over the snail that has been riding its arm since before the dungeon, like nothing happened. The quiet girl stood up from the fire and ran upstairs without a word to anyone. The machine watched her go and said it wasn't a normal kind of running. I believe it. I don't know what it was.

Then Aiph sat down with us and asked a favor. Don't tell them. She and the dragon lady built those tests together, and she doesn't want the dragon lady knowing it was hers that went wrong. Hey, we did it, she said, we crushed it, we're Uprooters now. And then she was gone again.

So that's what we are. Uprooters. I thought it would feel like more.

The others chewed on the asking for a long time. The machine doesn't understand hiding a truth and said so: leaving something out is still not the truth. The tall one wanted to know *why*. The cold one said he'd tell whatever lie Aiph needed, because she's the only one who hasn't looked at him like he's broken. I don't think it's a contest between the two recruiters, not the way the others were turning it over. Something got into her dungeon that she didn't put there. What she meant for us got killed by something she never meant for us at all. That's a hole in her fence. Nobody wants their hole in the fence talked about. So: don't say anything. That's easy. I'm good at not saying anything.

A dwarf came for drinks. I asked for wine and got red. The cold one asked for fire whiskey and the dwarf told him, kindly enough, that he didn't look like he could handle it. The machine asked for used cooking grease. When the dwarf came back he had a whole bottle of red and a whole bottle of the whiskey and a little wooden bowl of grease, and he said he was sorry, that he hadn't known who we were. Then he ran. People are going to start knowing who we are. I don't know how I feel about that yet.

The machine had been knitting something this whole time, and it turned out to be a bag that holds more than a bag should. The wizard and the tall one both looked at it like it had grown teeth. The wizard put our heavy gold in it. I'm glad not to be the one carrying it.

The other group came in behind the dragon lady. She told them to behave and have a drink on her, and then she went straight to Aiph at the bar. I kept to my seat. I said I'd keep my distance from that one and I meant it.

One of the other group, a woman who's sweet on the machine, sat down at our table and started asking it questions. I thought she was asking me. I stood up halfway to answer, and the cold one had to tell me she was talking to the machine about its snail. Fine. As far as I'm concerned we're lying, and everybody at that table was acting strange about it, so I'm done. I looked over at Aiph on my way up. She saw me stand. She looked away fast, like she hadn't. I only wanted her to see me go. I hope she remembers what we said to each other on the train.

I took the wine. All of it. Nobody needs to drink at me.

Outside, I found a place with something growing in it, like the last time I was here, and sat with my back to it. And it was wrong. I don't know how else to put it. This whole city is grown over: flowers down the walls, vines over the brick, leaves pouring off the corners of things. I know what a forest feels like. I know what a garden feels like. This isn't either one. It doesn't feel right, and I sat in it and couldn't make it feel right, and I didn't like how long I sat there trying.

Two of the other group came out of the tavern while I was sitting with that. Later, the wizard came out too, bolting through the doors like something was on fire. I don't know what he was running at. I didn't go after him.

I'm still out here. The bottle's lighter than it was. Whatever is happening at that bar, it's between the two of them, and I'll hear about it whether I want to or not.

*Leave it out, and it's still not the truth. The machine is right about that. I'll keep my mouth shut anyway.*

## Session Analysis
### Narrative Summary
Session 07 is a **joint crossover session** (both DMs, both parties, two players absent) set entirely at and around the **[[Hearthread Hall]]** in **[[Rhusatatiam]]**. It is a roleplay session with **no combat**, built around two parallel secrets: each recruiter asks her own group to keep its dungeon's events from the other. It ends on a cliffhanger stab.

**Opening (Group K).** Adam's recap of [[Session 06 - The Art of Staying Cool|Session 06]] places Group K back in Rhusatatiam, around the Common Fire "where the journey began," roughly early afternoon. Bebo, unfrozen, checks on its snail companion (Arcana nat 20: the snail is crawling up its forearm). [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]] bolts up from the fire and runs upstairs without a word (player absent); Bebo's Insight 18 reads it as clearly abnormal: "It's very clear something happened." Artie sits writing in his book, still stunned that his magic didn't work (the failed Identify from S06).

**Aiph's favor.** [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]] sits with the party and asks them not to tell Mags's group what happened in the dungeon: she and Mags "curated this carefully," and she doesn't want Mags to know "that mine was the one that [STT dropped word] up." The cover story is simply "We did it. We're great. We crushed it. We are now Uprooters." She leaves. The party debates it. Tobias recaps the dungeon as "The dragon killed what she put in and then the creepy crawler killed the dragon"; Zarna notes Aiph never told them what was supposed to be down there ("No," Adam confirms). Bebo objects ("Omission of truth is still not truth"); Zarna agrees but sees no choice; Bruin reads it not as rivalry but as embarrassment, since "something got in that she wasn't supposed to let in"; Tobias commits to any lie Aiph needs because she is "the only person who's not looked at me like I'm broken."

**Drinks.** Tobias orders a round (about a gold for the table). A tall **dwarven waiter** takes orders: wine for Bruin (red; Zarna asks for the bottle), dwarven fire whiskey for Tobias (the dwarf doubts he can handle it: "I can handle anything. Twice over."), and **used cooking grease** for Bebo. He returns with a full bottle of red, a full bottle of fire whiskey and the grease in a small wooden bowl, apologizes ("I didn't know who you were. That's my bad"), and leaves. Tobias's Insight 15: the dwarf thought the grease request was a joke but something made him feel he had to do it. Bebo greases its previously frozen joints and offers some to the snail, which scoots up to it. Tobias apologizes again for freezing Bebo; Bebo answers that it "believes in truth" and apologizes for making him emotional. Tobias chugs and refills; a Constitution save (19) has his hands go cold mid-drink before he steadies ("Just breathe. Don't freeze. Breathe.").

**Aiph waiting.** Zarna's Perception 12 spots Aiph at the end of the bar, staring at the entrance and checking "a device on her wrist," waiting for something (see Continuity Flags for a contradictory line).

**Bebo's bag of holding.** Bebo unveils a **bottomless folding bag for personal items** it has been knitting, sealing the threads with the last of the grease. Artie (Arcana 22) and Zarna (Arcana 17) recognize it as a bag of holding. Artie: "Bebo, you're like our own personal Derek. It's not gonna blow up, right?" Bebo warns about adding active explosives; Zarna keeps her exploding ball bearings out of it. **Artie gives Bebo the party's 600 gold**; the bag becomes the party inventory. Tobias puts in the **emerald** and keeps the **sapphire**. Bebo then begins flaying the **centipede eyeball** from the dungeon to extract the iris for **night goggles**; Zarna watches so intently she stops noticing anything else for most of the session. Bebo sends a sending-stone message to [[Nyx]]: "Bebo is asked to withhold truth. Bebo does not like this. Anxiously waiting your arrival."

**Group J arrives.** Mags leads Group J in, tells them "I will assume that you can all behave, enjoy your time, have a drink on me," and goes to Aiph at the bar. Their first exchange (witnessed by Aiph as a participant): Aiph, "We are in over our heads"; Mags, "Did your dungeon work the way you expected?" and "That only means he's getting stronger" (who "he" is is not stated); Aiph, "I don't know how."

**Crossover at the Group K table.** Lute finds Tobias ("Lute, you're alive"); each says they got the other something, then Lute leaves for the restroom. Nyx joins Bebo, admires the bag and the goggles project, and talks Bebo into naming its snail (Bebo had refused: "Too easy to die... Unnecessary heartbreak"). Both are from the Bloom, so the snail becomes **Bloom**; Bebo engraves the name on a wood chip and mends it to itself where the snail likes to sit. Nyx checks Zarna's armor for a paladin's emblem; Zarna "doesn't have one." Later, Bebo whispers that it has been told to lie and "does not lie to friends"; Nyx tells it she cares for it and feels responsible for it, and that "Sometimes we're given truths that are not ours to speak."

**Bruin leaves.** Asked what she's doing, Bruin is lost: as far as she's concerned, the group is lying and everyone is acting strange about it. Mistaking Nyx's question as aimed at her, she half-stands; Tobias clarifies it was for Bebo, and Bruin is gone. She stands on seeing Mags and tries to catch Aiph's eye (Perception 15): Aiph clocks her standing, tries to play it off, and looks away quickly when Bruin meets her eyes. Bruin goes outside ("cooped up indoors for long enough") to the same spot as last time, or a tree in some natural area. She **takes the bottle of red wine with her**. Outside, Alec calls a Nature check (6): "Rhusatatiam is a city of overgrowth," and to Bruin "it feels weird"; she knows forests and gardens, "And this just doesn't feel right." While she sits with it, two people from the other group exit the tavern.

**Artie, Dolm and Lute's note.** Artie visits Kidu's table to report that "a big fellow" from their group is three blocks down, crawling on the ground following a bug; Kidu: "Dolm does that sometimes. I wouldn't worry unless he isn't back by morning." Walking back, Artie crosses paths with Lute and catches her (Perception nat 20) slipping something into his bag: a note. It turns out to be a drawing of Lute flipping off the viewer; Artie passes it to Tobias, who laughs, folds it into his sketchbook and says "I really like Lute. She's kind of an odd duck."

**Lute at the bar (witnessed by Aiph).** Lute sits between Aiph and Mags, announces "I'm having an existential crisis," and gets a steamed milk. On a Perception nat 1 she misses Aiph's "we're relying on..." and Mags's "Don't make her mad." She then asks Mags to take her knives ("This isn't endwind. I'm not trying to steal Mags from you, Aiph"), handing over knives, a crossbow, a mace and a shield ("oh, they're real knives," Aiph says). Mags sends her off to drink her milk with her group; Auriron then walks Lute out for fresh air. From the Group K table, Tobias later says she "walked outside with that really intense dude from her team."

**The recruiters' argument (Aiph and Mags).** Mags returns to "us being in over our heads." Aiph: "They're not throwing weapons on a table saying, look at this." Mags: "There's something deeper with her," and she wants Lute in the group because "I could use it... I need her because she's a little unhinged." Asked what was in Aiph's dungeon, Aiph says something there was already dead "because something else showed up," and "We were so afraid after what happened last time that we waited too long... We are not in control anymore." Mags: "Yes, we are. We are in control. At least I am," and "As soon as they have any reason to believe that you and I are not in control, the whole house falls." Aiph, looking up at the onlookers: "I don't care if it happens to me." Mags: "I have to tell you something right now... You're not going to like it." Kidu's raven and Rowan's snake **Vesper** listen in (Group J familiars). At the Group K table, Tobias (Insight 24) reads it only as heated and contentious; Artie (Insight 4) and Zarna (Insight 12) read "tense." Tobias can't tell "if their conversation is arguing or very, very intense flirting."

**Table talk (Group K + Nyx).** Tobias worries Aiph has little faith in them ("She's getting me a teacher, a mentor. I'm. I'm pretty useless") and that he could be kicked off the team for nearly freezing the tavern; Zarna says Mags is the one with doubts. Tobias: "I just presume everyone's lying, everyone's judging, everyone's talking about you." Zarna finally notices Nyx (Perception 18 on a re-roll) after Nyx (Perception 11) has overheard fragments: that they were told not to tell, and that something went wrong in the dungeon. Bruin's bottle is noticed missing; Tobias corks the fire whiskey and stows it ("I have a dramatic childhood, I need this"). Nyx, over the sending stone, offers to "guard your truth" if concealment gets too heavy; Bebo answers that truth keeps people safe and **shows Nyx dungeon spoils**: a salvaged scale from the bag, the eyeball, and a vial of blood. Artie prompts "Tobias, show the heart"; Tobias: "No, that. That is mine." Zarna pulls a **giant curved rib** off her back. (In practice, Group K's secret is half-kept: no account of the dungeon is given, but the spoils are shown.)

**Lux (witnessed by Artie).** Outside, after Lute takes a hit from Auriron's pipe (a Group J scene), her alternate persona surfaces; Alec introduces "a character that we affectionately call Lux," shown to the table with **eyes a tinge of red and whiter hair**. Adam notes that "a certain person in my party... kind of knew what was happening and started charging towards it": **Artie bolts out of the tavern** (Bruin, outside, sees him run out). Artie and Lux exchange words (content held for review, see Archivist Notes, flag F65). Auriron leaves them to "some catching up," and Lux heads back inside for her weapons.

**The Nana letter (witnessed by Aiph).** At the bar, Mags tells Aiph this conversation "needs to matter. More than any other conversation we've ever had," unbuttons one of her many layers and slides a faded, crumpled envelope across the bar: **"To Aiph, from Nana."** The letter (read aloud; an in-world document) calls Aiph "Aiphie," says Nana and Aiph's "pop" were meant to hate each other but that hate turned into love, foretells that Aiph will tell a story about "taking out the bad guy" and be "Lady Aiph the hero," and says the person handing her the envelope will see the end of that story "Because I won't be here." Mags: she can't leave and never would, because she has to watch Aiph's ending. Aiph: "This doesn't change my mind... I want to kill him. I'm gonna kill him." Mags: "Would you still kill him if it meant you had to kill me too?"

**Group K disperses.** Tobias introduces himself to Nyx ("I am Tobias Wolfe"), leaves a charcoal-on-parchment sketch with her for Lute, and goes to find somewhere to sell his art: "I joined the Uprooters. I wanted to see the world. And this tavern is nice, but it's not the world." Nero jumps up onto him. To Zarna: "If you can't find me, Z, look for the water... I don't do fountains, just ponds." Bebo calls him "an odd duck" who "froze me solid"; Zarna defends him, and tells Bebo "Truth is a complicated thing." Zarna asks Nyx about Group J's dungeon: "Uneventful. We completed it... but we only got to keep one thing." Zarna admits she doesn't fully trust Aiph while Aiph withholds the whole truth; Bebo raises an arm: "Provision, trust." ("And the wise words of Bruin," Zarna adds.) Nyx says she trusts Mags "a great deal."

**The stab.** Lux, having magically returned her eyes to Lute's color, comes to the bar as Mags, holding Aiph's hand, asks: "if it was us, would you kill me?" Mags breaks off, agrees to return Lute's weapons if she goes straight upstairs, and notices how much more smoothly she puts her gear on ("Have you been practicing?"). Lux thanks her, turns toward the stairs, says "one more thing before I go," looks at Aiph, **and stabs Mags.** Session ends. **Mags's condition is not established** (above-table, Jordan: "We don't know that she's dead"; Adam echoed it).

**Table culture.** Big crossover energy: the "two big scary women" front line ("Female power baby"), the dwarf's escalating "this guy," Bebo ordering cooking grease, Kidu's steamed milk ("You're going wild tonight"), Rachel's string of low Perception rolls keeping Zarna oblivious to Nyx for an hour ("Three times"), and a table-wide groan at the cut to black ("Respectfully, I disagree. I think we have to go for another hour"). The next session (10/04/2026) is announced as joint again.
