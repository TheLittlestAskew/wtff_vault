## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

Session 02 (pipeline label) is a pre-campaign 1-on-1 character creation call between Adam (DM) and Taylor (player of [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan]]). The recording took place on May 4, 2026 — approximately six weeks before the campaign launched on June 14, 2026. No in-game events occurred. All content is OOC and above-table.

The call served two purposes: (1) Adam briefing Taylor on the world of [[Artemesia]] and the Uprooters, and (2) Taylor developing the concept for Bruin's character, race, class, origin, and inciting motivation.

**Character concept established this call:**
Taylor confirmed [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan]] as a Shifter Barbarian whose body is physically reacting to the [[Overgrowth]] — intensifying natural events across Artemesia — before anyone else in the world has named or noticed the phenomenon. This gives Bruin an intrinsic, physiological connection to the campaign's central mystery and a personal motivation to join the [[Uprooters]]: she feels something changing and wants to understand why. Adam endorsed this concept enthusiastically and noted it would directly inform world-building decisions he and Alec would make at their next collaborative meeting.

**World-building information shared:**
- The world is [[Artemesia]] — fully original homebrew; no existing D&D universe. [[Shmeetz]] made the map. Adam and Alec built the world collaboratively over several months.
- The post-Age of Heroes setting: heroes haven't existed for a long time; the world is overrun with unexplored nature.
- The [[Uprooters]]: a botanist-run organization that sends parties to explore dungeons unseen for eons. "Botany is a very sexy job in this world."
- The [[Overgrowth]]: the in-world term for intensifying natural events. Not yet named or noticed by most people at campaign start.
- Session 1 will be the characters' Uprooters interview; it will be both tables (WtFF + WtFR) simultaneously.
- A Session Zero was proposed for May 17 (did not take place at that date; campaign launched June 14).
- Adam plans to grant each character a unique item or ability once all characters are locked in. Taylor expressed interest in a swim speed tied to her Gulf-adjacent origin.

**OOC context:** Taylor discussed her recent job loss, her data archiving hobby (including having downloaded Pacts of Power roll data from DDB), and her grief at Pacts of Power ending. This campaign is, in part, a constructive new project for her during a transitional period.

**Session 1 spoiler context (OOC, above-table — NOT IC canon):** Adam confirmed that Session 1 and the final session are both planned as full-table joint events with both campaigns. The shared BBEG (OOC: "BBEG is the same") across WtFF and WtFR is confirmed.
