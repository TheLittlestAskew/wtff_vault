## Character POV Journal
### Isla "Bruin" Kaplan — In-Character Journal Entry

***It follows me, and I followed it here**

They put us in a room. Not the big noisy one downstairs — a smaller one upstairs, warm and a little messy in the way that means someone actually lives there. A blanket kicked off the couch. A wine glass still sitting out. The kind of mess that says a real person sleeps here, not a performance of a real person.

Aiph gathered us in. She had drinks brought up, and she asked us, one by one, why we were here and not somewhere else.

The machine speaks in third person. Said its name is something complicated — botanical, a whole phrase — and it loves water even though it cannot drink it. Said its heart fails it. There's something at the center of its chest that pulses like a slow ember. When it held up its hands to show me what I was trying to figure out when I picked it up, I noticed: the light from its center is the same color as its eyes. 

The wizard has a bird that can't die. He killed it to prove a point — sent it straight into a closed window — and it faded like smoke and came back whole. He held the cold man's hand, and his face did something I recognized: the brace against a thing that surprises you. Afterward he started scribbling in a little book.

The cold man. Tobias. He paints. He goes frost when he's frightened. His fox is the same way — alive past the point where alive should end. He seems not to know yet that this makes him remarkable. He thinks it makes him a problem.

The one who asks questions about old things. Zarna. She said she's a museum person, really, but said *archaeologist* first. She said it like she was trying it on.

And Eliza. Eliza was so quiet I almost didn't see her, and then something crossed her face when Aiph asked why she came, and I thought: that look. I know that look. Someone who came looking and is not ready to say looking for what.

When my turn came, I hesitated. Last time I answered that question I said something people didn't like. But Aiph caught it — she said *you've already passed the test*, and that was the thing I needed to hear. So I told her the only truth I know how to give: something is changing. Changing. I don't know what it is, but *it follows me, and I followed it here.*

She nodded. She seemed like she already knew.

Aiph told us about the hole at the center of this world. She said groups have gone before us — some didn't come back, some came back and didn't last much past coming back. She said we were chosen. She said we were extraordinary. I kept my head down.

Tobias asked about the Age of Heroes coming back. Aiph's face went flat in a way that wasn't cold — it was scared. *Heroes are good things in stories,* she said. *In actuality, all they bring is destruction.* 

When she left the room she stopped at the door. Turned. Looked at each of us. Said, *I need you to trust me. It can't come back.* Then she walked out.

We talked after. The machine — Bebo — let me hold it. It went completely still when I picked it up, like it was trusting me with the whole weight of itself, and it is very heavy. I set it down exactly where I found it. Bebo turned to face me and extended a limb. I reached out my hand. We shook. Its eyes went to the shape eyes make when something is glad.

Later there were more people. The other group came down to the tavern and everything got louder and more interesting and harder to track. Someone handed the cold man a piece of paper and he read it and a hand made of ice appeared in the air and then shattered. The dragon lady sat down next to him and asked if she scared him and he said yes and she got up and left. I think that was the point.

Bebo ended up with snails on it. I ate one. 

The other group's person — the one with the compass — cried outside in the dark. I didn't know that then. I only found out later from the way people moved around a door upstairs.

I sat outside for a while. Just breathing. The air was cold and clear and smelled of the wrong season, which is still something. I can work with something.

Morning. A platform. Two trains. We all stood there knowing this was the last time all twelve of us would be in the same place for a long while. The cold man and the compass girl made an agreement about marks — he paints a symbol on places he's been so his friends at home can trace him if they ever leave; she wanted to be part of the trail. I watched them press ink into each other's arms with their fingers.

The machine and the girl from the other group — Nyx — said goodbye to each other. Bebo tapped the place in its chest where the stone had sunk in. *Never be far.*

I don't know what Sunroute Crossing holds. I know that I'm going there with a machine that trusts me with its full weight, a wizard who kills his bird for science, a painter made of cold, a woman pretending she's only here by accident, and a quiet one who is looking for something. And me following a '*something*'.

Something is changing. I followed it here, and now I'm getting on a train.

## Session Analysis

### Narrative Summary

Session 02 was a **pre-campaign character-introduction session followed by a joint crossover tavern gathering**, picking up the same in-game evening as Session 01. No combat occurred.

**Part 1 — Group K Orientation (private room, ~01:08 of transcript):** [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]] escorted Group K — [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]], [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]], [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]], [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]], [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]], [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] — to a private upper-floor room at [[Hearthread Hall]]. The evening was warm and cozy (Aiph's personal space). Around and over drinks, Aiph invited each PC to introduce themselves and answer: *why are you here and not somewhere else?*

Introductions in order: **Bebo** — identifies as a botanical energy balance operator whose Heliolite core sometimes fails; wants to learn and survive. When asked what the core is made of, Adam searched his notes and confirmed the setting-canonical name *Heliolite* (spelled out in chat). A Nature check (Bruin: 12) confirmed the term is vaguely familiar but uncommon enough that even an educated person couldn't place it precisely. **Artie** — introduced as "Artie Veyr," asked Aiph if she knows a **Malus Thornwake**, saying that's why he's here. Aiph visibly paused, wrote a note, and responded in a way that implied the name registered; she also named Eliza (present at the table) as recognizing the name. **Eliza** — said she just wants answers; when Aiph said she "looks so familiar," Eliza denied knowing her (Persuasion/Deception: 19 — Aiph narrowed her eyes but let it go). **Bruin** — was visibly hesitant; Aiph sensed the anxiety (Insight: 8) and reassured her she'd already passed the test. Bruin then said: *"Something's changing. Changing. I don't know what it is, but I wanted to find out what it is, so I followed my nose."* **Tobias** — from the village of **Cardona** in the **Northern Roost Valley**; an artist with no magical training whose familiar, the arctic fox **Nero**, appeared off a canvas while he was painting and chanting something his friend Clara found in a book. Nero has never left and never disappeared. **Zarna** — actually a museum curator who assists her mentor with a museum on the Age of Heroes; came to the meeting by "happenstance," motivated by dungeon-diving possibilities.

Aiph then delivered the mission briefing: **The Uprooters** exist to investigate what's in **the Crater** at the center of [[Artemesia]] — she believes an entity there is growing more powerful and that it may precipitate another Age of Heroes, which she views as catastrophic ("heroes are good things in stories, Tobias, but in actuality, all they bring is destruction"). Groups A through I are unaccounted for (gone, or returned but didn't last). Group J (WtFR) will go to **Brindleberry Hollow**; Group K (WtFF) will go to **Sunroute Crossing** — both test dungeons. Tobias's Persuasion (25) unlocked this context about the Age of Heroes; a second Persuasion (21) confirmed that Mags is the person Aiph is waiting on romantically.

**Party socializing (free time in room):** [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] physically picked up Bebo — who went completely still like a rock — examined it, set it back down, and shook Bebo's hand (Bebo's eyes went to happy-slits). [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]] cast *Identify* on Nero after an Arcana check (17) confirmed it would work; he learned Tobias has somehow cast *Find Familiar* despite having no learned magic training, and Nero has never faded. Artie demonstrated by killing Orin (his own crow familiar) against a closed window — Orin snapped his neck, faded like the "Thanos snap," and reformed immediately, entirely nonplussed. Artie then took Tobias's hand (Arcana at advantage: 22) and felt ice-cold skin plus saw a faint blue magical aura surrounding Tobias. Bebo subsequently shook Tobias's hand — Con save (20): felt extreme cold, sensed danger. Bebo chose to hold on. Con save at disadvantage (9): failed — took 3 points of cold damage; ice formed on the limb. Tobias let go; Eliza cast *Healing Word* (die: 4, healing 4 HP). A Nature check by Eliza (19) and Arcana by Artie (18) established the phenomenon as elemental vulnerability — certain materials are more vulnerable to certain elemental magic. Bebo showed the group their pulsing Heliolite core (dull white light, same color as Bebo's eyes). Tobias mentioned he's been alive since birth with ice-cold skin, and his windows frost over when he nightmares. Bebo also told the group that on the road to town, a plant with green sludge and many mouths tried to eat them — they shocked it, healed it, and it let go.

**Break (10 minutes, ~01:08–01:20):** Both DMs reunited both parties in the joint session. OOC conversation during break included discussion of player locations and a hypothetical future in-person session.

**Part 2 — Joint Tavern Scene (~01:20–03:10):** [[Alec]] (DM of WtFR) narrated the two parties merging in the main tavern floor of [[Hearthread Hall]] — celebration, dancing, music. [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]] and [[Margaret "Mags" HoneyThatcher|Mags]] stood at the bar. Key beats:

- **Auriron approaches Nyx:** Auriron confronted [[Nyx]] about the symbol on her armor, asking which deity she follows. Nyx declined ("I don't know you well enough"). Auriron promised to keep learning about her and turned to [[Kidu]] to learn origami paper-folding.
- **Lute and Eliza:** [[Lute]] immediately sat in Eliza's face, asking rapid-fire questions about running into trees and stubbing toes. Eliza was overwhelmed.
- **Mags and Aiph at bar:** Mags told Aiph she's worried about "the two of them" (Nyx and Auriron): *"They might burn it all to the ground."* Aiph asked if Mags "would do what he did if…" — Mags said she couldn't be asked that and stormed off toward Lute.
- **Aiph pulls Lute aside:** Aiph tested Lute's compass — when Aiph held it, the needle pointed toward Mags. Aiph confirmed she'd had it pointing toward Mags consistently. She returned the compass, saying she believed it pointed toward what was most important in the moment.
- **Artie + Mags approach Tobias:** Mags accompanied Artie to see Tobias. Artie placed a napkin with the verbal components of *Mage Hand* in front of Tobias and asked him to read it aloud (Arcana: 21; Charisma: nat 20 / 25). Tobias articulated the words; a frost-spectral hand formed from ice, then claws appeared, he panicked, and the hand shattered on the ground. Mags sat next to Tobias and asked: *"Do I scare you shitless?"* He said *"Yes."* She stood and left.
- **Bebo and snails:** Bebo placed the escargot bowl on their head, distributed snails to Artie and Nyx, and wandered the tavern with snails crawling on their body, calling them "friends."
- **Nyx finds Bebo:** Nyx informed Bebo they won't be on the same team; Bebo said *"Bebo is sad."* Nyx revealed she is working on a way for them to stay in touch.
- **Mags gives Nyx communication stones:** Mags handed Nyx a small leather bag containing two crude, glowing stones, saying: *"Aiph and I acknowledge the connection between you"* (gesturing to Bebo). One stone for Nyx, one for Bebo. Mags also told Nyx: *"I did as you asked, and I reached out about your brother. I will personally see to it that he is taken care of."*
- **Bebo mends stone into chest:** Using *Mending*, Bebo set the communication stone into their chest where a heart would be. The rock formation caved in slightly, the stone sunk in, and small nubs of rock snapped around the edge to hold it — half-submerged, glowing.
- **Mags takes Auriron to balcony (private):** Mags told Auriron she chose him "because Aiph couldn't" and because he is "the fire." She issued a directive: if what the Uprooters are doing goes badly — if it changes things for the worse — Auriron is to "purify" and "make it all stop." She closed: *"Please don't fuck that up."* Auriron's Insight (nat 1) caused him to fully believe Mags might be capable of persuading a God.
- **Mags's vision on the balcony:** While smoking a black pipe alone on the balcony, Mags experienced a vision: she saw every tavern patron engulfed in flames — dancing, kissing, celebrating, a bard performing — all with skin ablaze and melting. The vision cleared; the scene returned to normal. She smoked faster and looked away.
- **Zarna + Nyx + Bebo:** Nyx showed Zarna and Bruin her caretaker notebook for Bebo's battery changes. Bebo identified Zarna and Bruin as the two most likely candidates to perform the procedure in Nyx's absence. Zarna felt she was within her skills. Bruin agreed but didn't understand the technical details. Nyx agreed to trust them, reluctantly. At this moment, Zarna rolled a Religion check (nat 20 / 21) while looking at Nyx — she saw vines, thorns, and shackles of ambient light on Nyx's armor, understanding it as the mark of a deity of justice. She also noticed a plume of black smoke rising from the balcony above, recognizing it as another person with religious devotion.
- **Rooms announced; Aiph and Lute:** Aiph received word from a tavern staffer that rooms were ready. Before moving, Aiph placed the compass on the table near Lute and told her she believed it must point to what's most important in the moment.
- **Aiph and Mags closing exchange:** Aiph found Mags leaving the balcony, kissed her, and said rooms were ready. Aiph paused and said: *"I would, by the way, do what he did. And that's why it needs to stop."* Then walked away.
- **Rowan:** Sought food at the bar; rolled Investigation (6) and heard something but couldn't locate the source. Alec gave him an origami snake as a gift before bed.
- **Dolm:** Found a platter of vegetarian food plus live escargot in shells; said a quiet murmur before eating; impressed by mushrooms despite disappointment at no meat. Carried a dilapidated orange plant in a cup up to his room and used Druidcraft (Arcana: 17) to help it slowly bloom in the dark — his window glowed until morning.
- **Eliza gave Lute a butterfly trinket** from her belt before going upstairs.

**Night (outside / hallway):**
Tobias went outside the tavern (rather than to his room) to paint his personal mark on the exterior wall — a tradition of marking places he's visited so his friends at home could trace him if they ever left. He asked a tavern staffer if there was an art supply shop nearby; Lute (Perception: 17 for Nero via D20) followed him, rolling Stealth (12) and being spotted by Nero. Lute approached. Tobias told her about his marking tradition. Lute offered to participate — they exchanged names (Lute confirmed she is named after the instrument), and Tobias added wings to Lute's symbol, saying his friends compared him to a sparrow for always looking toward the horizon. Lute gave Tobias ink she'd borrowed from Artie.

During this conversation, Lute experienced a **dark compulsion**: something in her head seized control, put her hand on her knife, and presented her with the thought of slitting Tobias's throat (D20: 8 — failed). She rolled again (12) — barely succeeded in not acting. She clawed at her own neck causing slight bleeding and teared up, then ran back inside crying. Tobias was bewildered and went back to painting.

Artie, still downstairs taking notes, noticed Lute run upstairs and heard a door slam. He rolled Perception (4 — failed to locate the room) then Perception again (23) and heard crying through a door. He knocked. [[Margaret "Mags" HoneyThatcher|Mags]] appeared from behind him, intercepted, and told him she'd handle it. Artie left.

Mags knocked on Lute's door. Lute refused to talk; Mags said *"I don't want to talk either"* and asked Lute to just open the door. Mags stepped in, closed the door, hugged Lute, and told a story: many years ago, Mags and Aiph navigated a dark forest together. Aiph admitted she was afraid of the dark. Mags stayed up all night to protect her — and was exhausted the following day when they encountered trouble, making Mags no help at all. *"In the end, it cost me. I need you to rest."* She made a pinky promise to sit outside Lute's door all night.

Aiph found Mags sitting in the hallway outside Lute's door. She brought two mugs. They sat together. Aiph said: *"She found someone else afraid of the dark."* Mags said she hated herself because she liked the WtFR party members: Dolm for his innocence, Kidu for the wisdom in a paper fold, Rowan for being bigger than his appearance, Auriron as "the fire," Nyx as "the light," and Lute — *"Lute is me. I think I really like them."* She admitted she feared losing them because losing them was real. Aiph replied: *"Some of my favorite things in the world are the things I'm afraid to lose. Have you ever loved something you weren't afraid to lose?"* Aiph briefly held Mags's hand before Mags pulled away. Aiph stood to leave, said: *"I honestly didn't expect them to intermingle as well. Perhaps they will teach us something."* Mags asked: *"Do you think I'm a Dragon Lady?"* Aiph immediately burst out laughing: *"Of course I do."* Mags told her to get away and go to sleep.

**Morning — Train Platform:**
All twelve adventurers gathered on a platform. Mags informed all of them (intentionally skipping Tobias, letting his groupmates relay the message) that trains would be departing early for their test dungeons. Group J (WtFR) toward **Brindleberry Hollow** (purple train); Group K (WtFF) toward **Sunroute Crossing** (green train).

Lute took ink she had borrowed from Artie and gave it to Tobias. Tobias and Lute pressed ink symbols into each other's arms. Tobias said *"Look out for sparrow"*; Lute said *"And I'll look out for a lute."* They made a pinky promise to be safe.

Nyx said goodbye to Bebo: *"It's been my greatest honor to take care of you."* Bebo tapped the stone now embedded in its chest. *"Bebo will miss you."*

Mags addressed Aiph before boarding: *"This is simple. I go to one place, you go to the other. We set the dungeons up. We know exactly what's in them… Keep them safe. Or don't."* Aiph kissed Mags on the forehead and turned back to the train: *"I think they're going to take care of us."* Both trains departed.

**Post-session OOC (≥03:10):** Both DMs expressed sincere emotion about the session quality. Jordan's alley scene with Lute was discussed — the table had watched in stunned silence. Adam noted that four or five moments in the session directly fed into Tobias's fear of being a problem for people, all organically. The WtFR group named their session *"Touched in the Womb."* Both DMs announced: groups separate into individual video channels from now until a planned future reunion.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Fear of loss as the cost of caring:** Mags's hallway monologue establishes that she genuinely loves her party and despises herself for it — loving them means being afraid to lose them, and losing them is a real possibility ("some of them will die"). - **Identity and self-construction:** Bebo wrestles with whether it is alive ("Bebo believes life is a matter of perspective"); Tobias thinks his magic makes him a problem, not a gift; Zarna introduced herself as an archaeologist and then corrected to museum curator. People are still deciding who they are.
- **Darkness held inside:** Lute's alley scene — something in her head nearly made her kill Tobias — establishes that at least one party member in this story carries a darkness that doesn't have a name yet. Mags's vision of the burning tavern suggests she may see the world differently than others.
- **Connection forged quickly and fiercely:** In a single evening, Bebo and Nyx said goodbye with the weight of a long friendship. Lute and Tobias made a pact in ink. These are people who recognize each other.
