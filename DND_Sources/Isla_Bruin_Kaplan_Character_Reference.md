## 2. Build & Mechanics

### Class progression
1. Barbarian 1–5
2. Fighter 1
3. Fighter 2
4. Return to Barbarian

**Totem picks:** Bear at level 3 **and** Bear again at level 6.

### Why the Fighter dip
- **Unarmed Fighting Style** is a cleaner route to a solid unarmed/grapple build than a full Monk multiclass.
- **Action Surge** adds a burst-damage option that fits her brawler identity.

### Rules system
- **Campaign baseline:** 2014 5e rules (per current project instructions — *not* 2024).
- ⚠️ **PENDING / reconcile:** Earlier build conversations referenced a *2014–2024 hybrid, adjudicated case-by-case with Adam.* The latest project instruction says **2014 only**. Worth a quick confirmation with Adam on which is authoritative for Bruin's edge cases.

### Build principles already locked in
- Wildhunt Shifter **already grants Perception and Survival** → background skill choices should avoid overlapping with those.
- Unarmed Fighting Style (Fighter) chosen over Monk multiclass for unarmed combat.

### Narrative grant from Adam
- **Swim speed** — granted as a story item once the character is finalized. No need to build around it mechanically.

## 3. Backstory

- Raised on the outskirts of the **Liriope River Net**, near the **Mouth that Feeds**.
- **Father and brother lost at sea.**
- **Mother is dead**
- Started fighting young, out of **survival** — not formal training.
- Father - Soren
- Mother - Sloane
- Brother - Wren

### Mentor — Calder Wynn
- A **blacklisted former sailor.**
- Witnessed a young Isla fight off a large waterway creature (the **Lirelle**, see §5) and began coaching her in **bareknuckle boxing and grappling.**
- Dynamic is **"Batman and Robin"** — Calder coaches, **Isla does the fighting.**
- ⚠️ **PENDING:** Calder's current status as an NPC in Adam's world.

### Narrative hook (from Adam)
- Isla's **Wildhunt physiology reacts to the Overgrowth before others can perceive it**, making her an **unwitting early-warning system.**
- **Inciting incident:** She **blacked out mid-fight** when one of these sensations overwhelmed her senses.

### Guilt thread
- The **Uprooters passed through her area once before**, while she was *already* experiencing these sensations — and **she said nothing.** This is a live source of internal conflict.

## 5. Connected Worldbuilding — The Lirelle

*Tayls' homebrew creature, designed to anchor Isla's backstory.*

- **Concept:** Semi-aquatic ambush grappler native to the **Liriope River Net.**
- **Look:** Wolverine–otter–eel hybrid.
- **Size:** Medium — ⚠️ **PENDING DM confirmation.**
- **Signature trait:** Color-shifting hide that reads water conditions and functions as a **living tide-gauge** for navigators.
- **Fighting style:** Grapple-and-death-roll — deliberately mirrors **Isla's** brawler identity.
- **Backstory link:** Isla learned to grapple by **outlasting a Lirelle's attack** as a child. Key reframing: the Lirelle was a **protective parent defending its young**, *not* a predatory aggressor. (This moral reframing meaningfully shaped both the creature and its cultural role.)
- **Overgrowth connection:** Deliberately left **ambiguous** for Adam to adjudicate.
- ⚠️ **PENDING:** Final **size and CR**; whether the Lirelle's Overgrowth behavioral thread connects to Isla's early-warning mechanic.
