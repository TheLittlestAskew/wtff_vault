## Combat

### Combat — Critical hits: double the dice or double the damage
**Ruling:** On a critical hit the player chooses, after rolling, whether to double the dice or double the damage. Adam: "I'm on Team Crit."
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [02:23:43]
**Context:** [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]]'s nat 20 maul attack on the [[Centipede-Snail-Fly Creature]]. (Earlier in the same fight the DM doubled the dice on the creature's own crit [02:06:49].)

### Combat — Savage Attacks on an opportunity attack
**Ruling:** Savage Attacks can be used on an opportunity attack.
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [02:17:18]
**Context:** [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]]'s opportunity attack as the creature fled behind the dragon's corpse.

### Combat — Breaking a grapple as a bonus action
**Ruling:** Breaking free of the creature's grapple was counted as a bonus action.
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [02:15:09]
**Context:** Bruin (grappled by the creature's dug-in claw) made a STR save of 25, tore the claw out, then still took her attack.

## Spellcasting

### Spellcasting — Detect Magic treated as continuous
**Ruling:** For brevity, Bebo's ritual Detect Magic was treated as continuously re-cast through the dungeon rather than tracked per cast.
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [01:50:03]
**Context:** [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]] re-reading magic schools in each layer of the test dungeon.

## Resting

### Resting — Level-up grants no long-rest benefits
**Ruling:** Leveling up between sessions does not grant the benefits of a long rest.
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [02:29:13]
**Context:** The party leveled from 1 to 2 at the end of S04, mid-fight, before "phase two" ("between now and our next part of this fight, you may all level up to level two" [02:29:00]).

## Exploration

### Exploration — Teaching Druidic answers needs no roll
**Ruling:** Bruin and Eliza could teach the others how to say the Druidic riddle answers without a roll.
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [00:25:50]
**Context:** Layer 1 riddle pillars: the answers had to be spoken in Druidic by four people at once.

### Exploration — Layer 3 potion: advantage on Wisdom saves
**Ruling:** The flower-cauldron potion grants advantage on Wisdom saving throws for one hour (and teleports the drinker onward).
**Session:** [[Session 04 — Four Blooms, Five Petals]] [01:39:19]
**Context:** The "four blooms, five petals" cauldron in Layer 3 of the test dungeon.

### Other — 2014 and 2024 sources are both allowed; the DM is final arbiter
**Ruling:** Adam: "we are going to try to chart an in between that makes sense of like 2014 and 2024. D&D... I will end up being the final arbiter of what feels the most fair. But more often than not, I'm probably gonna let it slide because I want to be able to, like, make very challenging combats." Taylor confirmed 10/10/26: both 2014 and 2024 sources are allowed, with the DM as the final arbiter.
