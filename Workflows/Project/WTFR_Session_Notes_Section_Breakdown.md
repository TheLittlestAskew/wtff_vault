# SESSION NOTES SECTION BREAKDOWN

This is a breakdown of the expectations for the output of the Where the Flowers Remember session notes. When asked to create session notes, this document shows what should be in the notes and how they should be arranged. Do not alter or skip sections. Tables must have enough rows to cover the full session. Capture every plot development with equal care — no chronological bias. Every event, roll, quote, and decision is tagged to the correct session date and character.

---

## [SESSION METADATA]

Generate a table (vertical key/value), in this order:

| Field | Detail |
|-|-|
| Campaign Name | Where the Flowers Remember |
| Session Number | [Formatted '01', '02', etc.] |
| Session Date | [MM/DD/YYYY] |
| Start Location | [Location at start of transcript] |
| End Location | [Location at end of transcript] |
| Party Present | [List of PCs/NPCs present] |
| Total Rolls Logged | [roll count] |
| Party Level | [Members indicated to be leveling up after this session] |
| Spelling Checked | [Yes/No] |

---

## [CHARACTER POV] — STORYTELLING EXCEPTION

Track the session through the lens of the POV character ([POV CHARACTER — name once your PC is defined]).

- **Storytelling wiggle room:** Unlike the rest of the objective archive, this section is output as an engaging, in-character story. Variance in wording and narrative flow is permitted to make it read as a true journal entry, provided the underlying events remain factually accurate to the session.
- **Personality engine:** Continuously analyze the POV character's actions and decisions and evolve the retellings to stay true to their established character.
- **Capture:** Emotional arcs, memories, shifting loyalties, temper, humor, anxieties, growth. Track the evolving meaning/use of signature items.
- **POV Journal Hard Limits apply** (see Project Instructions): no OOC speech, no above-table info, no metagame knowledge (dice numbers, spell names as mechanical labels, stats, levels, HP), no player process, no DM-rulings-as-rulings, no real-world names/session references. Test: *Could the POV character know, feel, or observe this from inside the story?*

---

## [SESSION ANALYSIS]

**[NARRATIVE SUMMARY]** — Detail all plot developments equally without chronological bias. Note unusual circumstances (split sessions, absent players, short run time). Include player-added flavor, vivid action, stylistic narration, and table culture/jokes.

**[SETTING]** — Session starting location and circumstances, and in-game time elapsed since the start of the story (if known).

**[LOCATIONS]** — All locations visited. Table:

| Location | Description | Notable Details |
|-|-|-|
| | | |

**[QUESTS / OBJECTIVES]** — Running list of open/unresolved threads, mysteries, political fallout, and storylines. Structure with parent threads and sub-threads. Mark "Completed (This Session)" when resolved.

**[SCENE / TIMELINE BREAKDOWN]** — A bulleted, chronological breakdown of scenes.

**[THEMES & EMOTIONAL BEATS]** — Recurring motifs and emotional arcs. Document dream sequences for implied symbolism (do not assert unconfirmed meanings).

---

## [CHARACTER ACTIVITY]

**[PARTY STRUCTURE & SUBGROUPS]** (table): Location | Characters | Objective | Status (Ongoing / Success / Failure). List each separate location and the specific characters present in that area.

**[NPCs]** (table): Name | Race/Class | Affiliations | Last Interaction | Last Known Location | Status (Dead / Missing / Captured / Imprisoned / In Hiding / On the Run / Unknown). Include all NPCs mentioned.

**[REPUTATION & RELATIONSHIPS]** — Evolving standing with factions, shifting alliances/rivalries, and changing internal party dynamics.

---

## [ARTIFACTS]

**[LOOT & ITEMS]** (table): Character/Owner | Item/Artifact | State/Context. Do not invent item properties.

---

## [LOGS]

**[ENCOUNTERS]** (table): Enemies | Location | Party/Allies Present | Trigger | Outcome.

**[INITIATIVE]** (table, one per combat): Character | Initiative Roll | Turn Order. Infer enemy placement from turn order if unstated. Opportunity attacks are NOT an initiative change.

**[ENCOUNTER SUMMARY]** — Brief summary of results and implications after the tables.

**[FULL ROLL LOG]** (table): Character/NPC | Roll/Check | Result | Context/Outcome. Attribute every roll to the correct PC/NPC. Background-fill rows associated with combat/initiative rounds. Identify individual and party-wide roll trends. (WTFR roll archive is not wired until a live `game_id` exists — until then, log results confirmed in the transcript, marked `physical/verbal`.)

---

## [QUOTES & LANGUAGE]

**[QUOTE BOARD]** — Verbatim quotes only, tagged by character and type (Funny / Poignant / DM Quip / Banter / Serious / Important to story).

**[PROFANITY RECORD]** (table): Speaker | Curse Word | Frequency | Context. One row per speaker/word combo.

**[ALTERNATE TITLE OPTIONS]** — 5 potential session titles (Humorous, Dramatic, Serious, Straightforward, Quote-based). Record the final chosen title once confirmed by the user.

---

## [ARCHIVIST NOTES]

**[PATTERNS, PROGRESS & FUTURE IMPLICATIONS]** — Patterns in tactics or story motifs, character growth arcs. No metagaming or future-plot prediction from published lore.

**[CONTINUITY FLAGS, MISSING INFO & AMBIGUITIES]** — All `[inaudible]` segments, transcript-only rolls, unresolved attributions, and suspected spelling issues needing confirmation.
