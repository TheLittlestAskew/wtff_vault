# Zarna Morganach

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* An almost **7-ft half-orc** with an **inviting (not imposing) face**.
- *(S01)* **Oversized, face-wide glasses**; carries a **large hammer + short sword** and a tool pack. "Prepared for an adventure."
- *(S07)* **No paladin emblem** on her armor ([[Nyx]] looked: "She doesn't have one").
- *(S07)* Carries a **giant curved rib** (dungeon loot) on her back.
- *(S07)* The dwarven waiter calls her a "big armor scary woman."
- *(S04)* **"A giant half orc," not stealthy;** fights with a **maul ("a giant hammer")**.

## Backstory
- *(S01)* Only learned of [[The Uprooters]] **that morning, from a flyer**.
- *(S01)* Drawn by **history, artifacts, and dungeon-diving**.
- *(S02)* Actually **a museum curator** who assists a mentor with a museum on the Age of Heroes. She introduced herself as "archaeologist" first and then corrected to museum curator — she said it "like she was trying it on."
- *(S02)* Came to the Group K orientation by **"happenstance"**; motivated primarily by dungeon-diving possibilities.
- *(S05)* Has a **mentor she "still kind of work[s] for"**; **a traveling museum** that goes from place to place was her home; she traveled as a young adult; she **focuses on old weaponry** [01:55:20]–[01:56:41].
- *(S05)* Has met a few others who study the era of Heroes, none notable (Adam) [01:56:41]. Adam calls her "a glorified museum curator" [02:17:19].

## Personality
- *(S01)* **Studious, curious, principled** — frames good and evil around the question of whether doing evil to do good can really be good.
- *(S02)* **Trying on identities** — said "archaeologist" before correcting to museum curator. Possible that she's still working out who she is in relation to the work she does.
- *(S02)* **Loudly sipped beer** during Aiph's Age of Heroes speech — a small, dry, physical comedy that reads as either discomfort or dry humor.
- *(S02)* Rolled Religion nat 20/21 on Nyx's armor — observed divine illumination (vines, thorns, shackles) and a "deity of justice" reading. Also noticed the plume of black smoke from the balcony above, recognizing it as another person with religious devotion.
- *(S03)* **Treasures gifted art** — carefully pocketed [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]]'s hammer sketch on her person.
- *(S03)* **Pragmatic in the trust debate** — "I'm with the robot"; readily backed [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]]'s "provisional trust."
- *(S07)* **Becomes completely absorbed watching Bebo craft** — missed [[Nyx]] sitting at her own table for most of an hour.
- *(S07)* **Trusts Aiph "to a certain extent"** but not fully while information is withheld.
- *(S07)* "**Truth is a complicated thing.**"
- *(S04)* **Mercy-kill line** over the wounded creature: "I guess I have to put you out of your misery."
- *(S04)* Wary of the objective: "something's wrong with the chest"; hopes the dragon "wasn't her pet."
- *(S05)* **Wants dragon bone for a weapon**; a "loot goblin without being a rogue" ("we are tearing this good luck dragon apart").
- *(S05)* **Fascinated by Bebo's craft:** "From one craftsman to a craftsman to another."
- *(S05)* **Lectures on upkeep:** told Aiph "regular dungeon maintenance is important."

## Abilities & Class Features
- *(S04)* **Languages:** Common and Orc.
- *(S04)* Darkvision (among those rolling Perception in the dark in Layer 1).
