# Aiphrumfite "Aiph"

> WtFF-side recruiter NPC for [[The Uprooters]]; voiced by [[Adam]] (the WtFF DM). This page **is** WtFF canon (unlike the crossover WtFR cards). Transcript STT variants "Am from Fright / Aeth / Eeth / Eighth / Ava / Eiff / 8th" all normalize to **Aiphrumfite / Aiph**.

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* A chaotic, tinkering, alchemist-presenting figure who stumbles in carrying an overflowing duffel of oddments. Constantly tinkering and mixing as she talks.
- *(S01)* Carries a pinch/bag of **sand that ignites in her fingers and changes color** (and changes again), a stoppered bottle, a small potted plant, chalk, and crumpled, water-stained notes.
- *(S07)* Wears a **device on her wrist** that she keeps checking while watching the tavern entrance.
- *(S05)* Waited at the dungeon's surface in a **pull-out chair under a tent she conjured**, notepad and brush in hand, staring at the trapdoor.
- *(S08)* At the festival: **"beautiful curly red hair flowing all the way down,"** a **deep green sequined dress with a long slit** [01:51:53]; an untouched drink; watching the dancers, "it looks like she wants to smile but just can't."
- *(S08)* After the dome, **covered in Mags's blood** ("covered in my. In Mags's blood"); left to clean up.

## Backstory
- *(S01)* Shares a long, fraught, intimate history with the WtFR recruiter [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] — they reference a past fight ("the last time we had a fight and someone convinced you otherwise") and claim together to "know the last hero to ever exist." Details [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- *(S07, in-world letter)* [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] gave her a faded envelope **"To Aiph, from Nana"**, read aloud at the bar. Per the letter: her Nana called her **"Aiphie"** and **"Lady Aiph"**; Nana and Aiph's **"pop"** were meant to hate each other, and that hate turned into love; the letter foretells Aiph telling a story about "taking out the bad guy." Recorded only as an in-world document; Nana's and pop's status are [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- *(S06, per the written recap)* **She and [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] love one another** (the DM-written recap frames Tobias's question as whether they "were looked down upon for loving one another"). Canon per the recap; earlier player speculation is not the source.
- *(S06, per the written recap)* There is **"a place which I never hope to return to one day."** Where it is, and why, are [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- *(S08)* **She and Mags each chose their group**; Aiph: "Once they pass the test, I send them on their way... Mags and I recognize that this could be different." Mags, on choosing Lute: "That's why I chose her, Aiph. You know about mine. Is that why you chose me?" (the "Aiph" is a 50% correction; "You know about mine" not interpreted, F11).
- *(S08)* **She and Mags "have a common goal"** but go about it "very, very differently"; "neither of us has been able to convince the other they're right." Adam (above-table DM framing): what is in "the crater" in the center is "ruining Artemesia," and the recruiters "go about it differently."

## Personality
- *(S01)* Mercurial, blunt, and volatile; affectionate-but-lethal toward [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]]. Wagered that 5 candidates would "make it" (Mags guessed 2).
- *(S01)* Values "odd ducks" — outsiders — as the ones who "move the timeline."
- *(S02, catch-up — page was blocked from S02 edits by the Unicode filename; added retroactively S03)* Reads and remembers individuals sharply; her fear presents as a sudden *flatness* rather than panic; references an unnamed "he" whose past action is why the Age of Heroes "can't come back." (See the S02 note and Open Threads "Who is 'he'?")
- *(S03)* **Genuinely invested in Group K** — told them plainly she's "rooting for you all" and is "a lot more hopeful than a day or two ago," even as part of her still doubts they're ready. Blunt about lethal stakes ("has anyone died? — yeah, of course").
- *(S03)* **Curates her own test dungeon** — personally built the five-layer dungeon at Sunroot Crossing ("some of it is dangerous, some simulates danger"); waits at the surface under a tent.
- *(S03)* Carries a **broken pocket watch** and finds it funny.
- *(S07)* **Protective of her own failure:** asked Group K not to tell Mags's group what happened in her dungeon ("that mine was the one that [inaudible/cut off] up"); cover story: "We are now Uprooters."
- *(S07)* **Admits loss of control** to Mags: "We are in over our heads"; "We are not in control anymore"; "I don't care if it happens to me."
- *(S07)* **Wants to kill an unnamed "him":** "I want to kill him. I'm gonna kill him." Identity [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- *(S05)* **Celebratory, then visibly shaken:** clapped and grabbed Zarna's hand at the party's return, then paced once she saw Tobias's drawings; **leaves rather than explain** (downed Bruin's and Eliza's shots and went to the rooms) [01:26:36]–[01:37:42].
- *(S05)* **Has never seen either creature** from her own dungeon, and has never walked every room "because most groups don't. Don't make it all the way through." Adam (above table): she is not stunned that they survived; she is stunned that she doesn't know what either creature is [01:40:00]–[01:40:41].
- *(S08)* **Asks rather than threatens:** "I will not extend such a threat. I'm not forcing you to do this, but I believe it's the best course of action. And I believe in all of you." Accepted Bebo's conditional yes as "fair."
- *(S08)* **Angry, then gentle:** to Lute in the dome, "now is the moment in which you supply a good explanation... or beg for your life"; "I like Lute. I don't like whoever the fuck that was that stabbed my mags." Lute's confusion cut through her anger.
- *(S08)* **Draws the group line:** on Lute/Lux, "It is whatever it is. Group J's problem. It's not our problem"; "there's a reason I didn't choose that particular prospect for this group. But Mags makes her own decisions."
- *(S08)* **Undecided:** whether she travels with Group K, "I have not made my decision yet."

## Abilities & Class Features
- *(S01)* Demonstrated magic: floated the brawling candidates Tessil Goldpetal & Pella Bramblewine apart and ejected them. Class/abilities otherwise [Unknown/Ambiguous].
- *(S08)* Inside the dome: held Lute frozen (failed WIS save), **removed the knife and healed Mags's wound with green magic**; conjured a daisy or daffodil petal as a drink garnish. No spell named. Her Insight lost to Lute's nat 20.

## Relationships
- **↔ [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]]:** deep, volatile partnership — mutual love and mutual lethal threat ("This is only funny because I'll kill you too"). They split the candidates, each taking a group.
- **↔ [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]]:** specifically wanted Bruin for [[The Uprooters]] — partly the shared "scent," partly "the energy they gave." Selected her into Group K despite Mags's warning. *(S03)* Confided the truth of Bruin's scent — the world *shifting*, Mags shifting too — and that **violence is coming; that's part of why she chose Bruin.** Accepted Bruin's boundary re Mags, promising only to *try* to intercede.
- **↔ [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] (S03):** the rift is reaffirmed — [[Orin]] overheard the two "not on the same page about what this group does," and Aiph's "I don't actually think any of them are good enough." Aiph says Mags is "shifting" too and hates change; Mags is "stronger than she is."
- **↔ [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] (S07):** The rift escalates into an open argument at the [[Hearthread Hall]] bar over control and over [[Lute]] (Mags wants Lute "because she's a little unhinged"). Mags gave her the envelope from Nana and said she can't leave "and I never would." Aiph: "This doesn't change my mind." Mags: "Would you still kill him if it meant you had to kill me too?" Mags was holding Aiph's hand when Lux stabbed her in front of Aiph. **Mags's condition is unknown.**
- **↔ Group K (S05):** "you did it so much faster than Mags's group"; "I chose the best group." Passed them ("Congrats. You passed the test") but called the two unknown creatures "more concerning whether or not Group K pass the test." Zarna lectured her on dungeon maintenance; Artie: "Did you put a fucking dragon in there?" ("You were supposed to kill it, so,"). Left Bruin's question about earlier groups unanswered. Tobias: "Her reaction says she expected us to die."
- **↔ Group K (S07):** Entrusted them with keeping her dungeon's failure from Group J and told them they're Uprooters. Looked away quickly when [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] sought her eye. Is getting [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] "a teacher, a mentor" (per Tobias).
- **↔ [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]] (S06):** Received his apology for freezing the tavern's patrons and told him his powers aren't a burden nor a liability but **an asset**: if he stops running from it and stops trying to control it, and lets it flow through him, he may find a power he didn't know he had, one [[The Uprooters]] may need one day. On the overnight train he confided in her about his home town; she answered that people who look down on others project "their own lack and their own pains," and **struck a deal**: she has a place she never hopes to return to, so "You can go to my place for me and I to your place for you."
- **↔ [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] (S06):** **They love one another** (DM-written recap). A series of messages passed between them as Group K's train got a head start back to [[Rhusatatiam]]; content not stated.
- **↔ [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] (S08):** Mags survived; inside the dome Aiph healed her. "We can't just jump out of this dome and all Willy Nilly with 12 people. You have to take your group and I have to take mine." "Sometimes really, really don't like you," then "but I do always love you" (Adam's pronoun slip, F11). At the festival bar: "We don't want the same thing"; "change my mind. Convince me Aiph Make me see it your way" (direction unclear, F18); "the Next time we see each other, it's not as adversaries, but it's together." Mags: "Do you think at the end of it, you and I are the same?" Aiph, forehead to forehead: "I don't know if we'll ever be the same, but we will always be one." They kissed; she walked away and left **a ring** in Mags's hand. Joked they should "fire him [Dolm] from the uprooters."
- **↔ Group K (S08):** Split the groups ("I need Group K to come with me"); at the riverbank asked them to keep "our business our business" (all six agreed; Bebo conditionally), disclosed Mags's expulsion threat, named [[The Burn]] as the next destination, left them the dragon heart, and promised items before they leave. "We, all of us now are uprooters." At the festival watched [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]], [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]] and [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] from the bar.
- **↔ [[Lute]] (S08, crossover):** Removed the blade and confronted her; lost an Insight contest to Lute's nat 20; released her. "I like Lute."
- **↔ WtFF party (Group K):** now their recruiter/leader — [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]], [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]], [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]], [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]], [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]], [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]].
- **↔ [[Eliza Duskbloom|Eliza]]:** uncannily feels he *knows* her, despite never meeting; Mags silently signaled him to pick her.
