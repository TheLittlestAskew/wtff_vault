# Tobias Wolfe

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* **Tall**; dark hair across the face; **piercing blue eyes**.
- *(S01)* **Cobalt paint** on hands, neck, and face; a **sapphire pendant** on a silver chain.
- *(S01)* **Arcane runes / spell-circles tattooed** on his arms.
- *(S01)* His white fox **[[Nero]]** rides on his shoulders.
- *(S02)* Carries a **blue paint-spattered ace of hearts** in his vest pocket — a personal talisman used to "steel his nerves."

## Backstory
- *(S01)* His **village thought him strange**; he never felt the communal belonging others did — a self-described outsider / **"odd duck."**
- *(S01)* Travels with his companion [[Nero]].
- *(S02)* From the village of **[[Cardona]]** (≤60%) in the **[[Northern Roost Valley]]** (≤55%). Friends there call him "sparrow" because he is always looking at the horizon.
- *(S02)* His companion [[Nero]] **appeared from a painting** while Tobias was chanting words his friend Clara found in a book — Tobias has never trained in magic. Nero has never faded or disappeared.
- *(S02)* Has had **ice-cold skin since birth**; his windows frost over when he has nightmares. His passive cold aura is a faint blue, visible to magical senses (Artie: Arcana 22 at advantage).
- *(S02)* **Tradition:** paints a personal mark at every place he visits so his friends in Cardona can trace him if they ever leave. The mark is a **sparrow symbol**. He asked about nearby art supply shops to restock his paints.
- *(S03)* **Carries a secret truth** he "can't tell them yet" — confided to [[Nero]] that the "I summoned Nero with a spell" story is **a cover**.
- *(S03)* Describes his upbringing as "**whispers, looks, judgment, no trust**" — the wound underneath his distrust of the recruiters and of near-strangers.
- *(S07)* "**I've only ever been in one city**" before [[Rhusatatiam]] and the dungeon town.
- *(S07)* "**I have a dramatic childhood**" (said while corking the fire whiskey).
- *(S07)* [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]] is getting him **"a teacher, a mentor."**
- *(S07)* His **hands go cold when he drinks** (fire whiskey, CON save 19) — continues the ice thread.

## Personality
- *(S01)* **Naive, gentle**; an artist who wants to "see it all… live it all" and to **"find home."**
- *(S02)* **Fears his magic makes him a problem for people** — this pattern was noted explicitly by Adam post-session: four or five S02 moments organically clustered around this wound (Bebo's cold damage, Eliza interceding, Mags's "do I scare you shitless" approach).
- *(S02)* **Turns toward Zarna as the safe/non-threatening party member** — after the Mags encounter, he went to Zarna.
- *(S03)* **Gifts art** — drew and gave [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] a sketch of her hammer; also sketched [[Delilah]] as a "tip."
- *(S03)* Enters an artistic **"fugue state"** when drawing (Performance 26).
- *(S03)* **Distrust rooted in exclusion** — "or they stay strangers and outcasts"; distrusts the recruiters (via [[Orin]]'s overheard "I don't think any of them are good enough") and questions trusting near-strangers, but ultimately (and slowest) lays his hand into the "provisional trust" pact.
- *(S07)* **Loyal to Aiph:** "the only person who's not looked at me like I'm broken"; will tell "whatever lie she needs."
- *(S07)* "**I just presume everyone's lying, everyone's judging**"; "I've always kind of been justifying my existence to everyone around me."
- *(S07)* Calls [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] **"Z."**
- *(S07)* **Drawn to water:** "If you can't find me, Z, look for the water... I don't do fountains, just ponds."
- *(S07)* Stayed up painting; **fears being kicked off the team** (for nearly freezing the tavern).

## Relationships
- **↔ [[Nero]]:** his white-fox companion, who fetched him food during the mingling. Nero served as perimeter lookout outside the tavern (S02); detected Lute approaching at Perception 17.
- **↔ [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph’|Aiph]]:** Aiph’s "odd ducks moved the timeline" speech was directed at him; selected into Group K.
- **↔ [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]]:** *(S02)* Artie cast Identify on Nero; took Tobias’s hand (Arcana 22) to examine the aura. Dynamic: Artie is the scientist, Tobias is the subject.
- **↔ [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]]:** *(S02)* Bebo shook his hand and took 3 cold damage; Tobias let go and told Bebo he wasn’t liable.
- **↔ [[Margaret "Mags" HoneyThatcher|Mags]]:** *(S02)* Unsettling encounter — Mags accompanied Artie, sat next to Tobias, asked "do I scare you shitless?", got a "yes," stood and left. He reads her as deliberately intimidating.
- **↔ [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]]:** *(S02)* He turns toward Zarna as the safest/least-threatening party member. *(S03)* Strengthening — gifted her a sketch of her hammer (she pocketed it carefully); opened small talk about her Age-of-Heroes expertise.
- **↔ [[Nero]]:** *(S03)* Confided to Nero over sweets that he "can't tell them the truth yet" and that the "summoned you with a spell" story is a cover — the session's clearest window into his secret.
- **↔ [[Delilah]]:** *(S03)* Sketched her (Performance 26) as a "tip" at the [[X Marks the Spot Inn]].
- **↔ [[Lute]]:** *(S02)* New bond via ink exchange and pinky promise on the train platform; Tobias’s sparrow mark + Lute’s mark on each other’s arms. "Look out for sparrow / And I’ll look out for a lute." *(S07)* Affectionate reunion ("Lute, you're alive"); gifts traded (her drawing via Artie; his sketch left with Nyx). "I really like Lute. She's kind of an odd duck"; notices she gets "fidgety" and "flighty" around him: "She's like a cat."
- **↔ [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]] (S07):** Deepened loyalty — will tell "whatever lie she needs." Worries she has little faith in him ("She's getting me a teacher, a mentor. I'm. I'm pretty useless").
- **↔ [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]] (S07):** Apologized again for freezing Bebo; Bebo accepted on its own terms ("Bebo believes people should face truth") and later called him "an odd duck" who "froze me solid."
- **↔ [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] (S07):** She talked him down from self-doubt; he calls her "Z."
- **↔ [[Nyx]] (S07, crossover):** Introduced himself ("I am Tobias Wolfe") and left his sketch for Lute with her.
