# Tobias Wolfe

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* **Tall**; dark hair across the face; **piercing blue eyes**.
- *(S01)* **Cobalt paint** on hands, neck, and face; a **sapphire pendant** on a silver chain.
- *(S01)* **Arcane runes / spell-circles tattooed** on his arms.
- *(S01)* His white fox **[[Nero]]** rides on his shoulders.
- *(S02)* Carries a **blue paint-spattered ace of hearts** in his vest pocket — a personal talisman used to "steel his nerves."
- *(S05)* His **arm tattoos glow faintly blue, turning orange** as he manipulates a spell [00:33:33].
- *(S05)* **Black hair** and **"very deep sapphire" blue eyes** (his own account of standing out in a town of blond and red-haired humans) [02:16:22], [02:47:17].
- *(S05)* **Left-handed** [00:11:02].

## Backstory
- *(S01)* His **village thought him strange**; he never felt the communal belonging others did — a self-described outsider / **"odd duck."**
- *(S01)* Travels with his companion [[Nero]].
- *(S02)* From the village of **[[Cardona]]** (≤60%) in the **[[Northern Roost Valley]]** (≤55%). Friends there call him "sparrow" because he is always looking at the horizon.
- *(S02)* His companion [[Nero]] **appeared from a painting** while Tobias was chanting words his friend Clara found in a book — Tobias has never trained in magic. Nero has never faded or disappeared.
- *(S02)* Has had **ice-cold skin since birth**; his windows frost over when he has nightmares. His passive cold aura is a faint blue, visible to magical senses (Artie: Arcana 22 at advantage).
- *(S02)* **Tradition:** paints a personal mark at every place he visits so his friends in Cardona can trace him if they ever leave. The mark is a **sparrow symbol**. He asked about nearby art supply shops to restock his paints.
- *(S03)* **Carries a secret truth** he "can't tell them yet" — confided to [[Nero]] that the "I summoned Nero with a spell" story is **a cover**.
- *(S03)* Describes his upbringing as "**whispers, looks, judgment, no trust**" — the wound underneath his distrust of the recruiters and of near-strangers.
- *(S07)* "**I've only ever been in one city**" before [[Rhusatatiam]] and the dungeon town.
- *(S07)* "**I have a dramatic childhood**" (said while corking the fire whiskey).
- *(S07)* [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph‘|Aiph]] is getting him **"a teacher, a mentor."**
- *(S07)* His **hands go cold when he drinks** (fire whiskey, CON save 19) — continues the ice thread.
- *(S04)* Names his **headmistress** for the first time, looking at his feet: **"headmistress Vale"** ("Hey, she was a saint." / "She just wanted me to be safe"). Her saying: the devil "usually arrives at the front door in a suit and a smile." Adam: "You would not have heard that name before now" [01:41:03]–[01:41:46]. *(Name ≤60% first-seen; no link to Lethira Vale asserted.)*
- *(S04)* **"Misspent youth. Don't judge."** (said after pickpocketing Artie). Adam: "You're used to moving slowly and quietly, not wanting to be noticed."
- *(S05, "the bloody truth")* **Left on a fountain** "in the middle of the harshest winter on record"; **found by a priest** and given to an **orphanage**, where "the only person who looked out for me was the headmistress"; she gave him sketchbooks and charcoal and pulled them out of the fountain when other kids threw them in [02:46:23]–[02:47:17]. *(The unnamed headmistress is likely S04's "headmistress Vale"; not merged until confirmed.)*
- *(S05)* An **outsider** with black hair and blue eyes in "a town full of blond red headed humans"; called a **demon** and a **monster** [02:47:17].
- *(S05)* His **magic woke when he fell out of a tree and broke his arm**; one of his "two maybe, friends" carried him into town and "The town literally froze behind our footsteps" on the way to "his father's carpentry shop" [02:15:00], [02:47:17]. *(No contradiction asserted with S02's Nero-from-a-painting origin: first magic vs. Nero's origin; confirm with Seth.)*
- *(S05)* His magic answers emotion: "When I'm in pain or when I'm upset or when I'm sad, I bring forth winter" [02:47:17].
- *(S05)* His friends were "deemed tarnished"; when he asked them to come on this journey, "they chose their roots over new horizons." He screamed at those who stood up for him before leaving [02:47:17].
- *(S05)* An **archaeologist** ("she") who specialized in the era of Heroes **taught him Elvish** and told him the story of a singer who moved the god of death [01:56:08]–[01:58:43].
- *(S05)* **No formal magic education:** a spell circle and rune appear in his mind, and he draws it [02:15:00]–[02:15:20].
- *(S06, per the written recap)* **Treated poorly in his home town "for who he was."** He told Aiph so and asked whether she and Mags were ever looked down upon for loving one another.
- *(S06, per the written recap)* There is **someone in his home town he loves very deeply and misses**; still, he says **he will never again set foot there.**
- *(S06, per the written recap)* At a pond near [[Sunroute Crossing|Sunroot Crossing]] he saw in the water **not his own reflection but that of someone dear to him**, and spoke aloud to those from his past: how he hates them, loves them, misses them. The reflection dissolved back to his own. Who he saw is not stated.

## Personality
- *(S01)* **Naive, gentle**; an artist who wants to "see it all… live it all" and to **"find home."**
- *(S02)* **Fears his magic makes him a problem for people** — this pattern was noted explicitly by Adam post-session: four or five S02 moments organically clustered around this wound (Bebo's cold damage, Eliza interceding, Mags's "do I scare you shitless" approach).
- *(S02)* **Turns toward Zarna as the safe/non-threatening party member** — after the Mags encounter, he went to Zarna.
- *(S03)* **Gifts art** — drew and gave [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] a sketch of her hammer; also sketched [[Delilah]] as a "tip."
- *(S03)* Enters an artistic **"fugue state"** when drawing (Performance 26).
- *(S03)* **Distrust rooted in exclusion** — "or they stay strangers and outcasts"; distrusts the recruiters (via [[Orin]]'s overheard "I don't think any of them are good enough") and questions trusting near-strangers, but ultimately (and slowest) lays his hand into the "provisional trust" pact.
- *(S07)* **Loyal to Aiph:** "the only person who's not looked at me like I'm broken"; will tell "whatever lie she needs."
- *(S07)* "**I just presume everyone's lying, everyone's judging**"; "I've always kind of been justifying my existence to everyone around me."
- *(S07)* Calls [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] **"Z."**
- *(S07)* **Drawn to water:** "If you can't find me, Z, look for the water... I don't do fountains, just ponds."
- *(S07)* Stayed up painting; **fears being kicked off the team** (for nearly freezing the tavern).
- *(S04)* **Always carries "a pencil or a paintbrush or a piece of charcoal";** sketches even mid-danger (a rushed sketch of the dragon's corpse, Performance 24).
- *(S04)* His **dancing lights are shaped like little birds**; his spell visuals are **circle runes that turn into chalk, then ink, then paint**.
- *(S04)* Asks [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] if he may call her **"Z"**; tells [[Nero]] to go to Zarna if anything happens to him.
- *(S04)* **Secretly moves potions to others** (lifted one of Artie's for Zarna; later slipped "his potion" into Artie's bag).
- *(S04)* **Paints a symbol on a dungeon wall** (Layer 3) before drinking the potion.
- *(S04)* Cautious: panicked that the ladder was gone; gagged himself with a handkerchief in the noise-trap room; stopped Zarna walking into the corpse.
- *(S05)* **Two modes:** in combat and while drawing he goes instinctive, relaxed, "hyper confident and focused" (Adam), against his usual skittishness. "maybe Tobias is not who you all thought he was" [00:11:28], [02:38:39].
- *(S05)* Calls himself **"a jeweler"** [02:11:18]; "I'm not real good with people."
- *(S05)* **Makes an ice rose** for [[Delilah]].
- *(S05)* **Guards his past** ("you don't want Bebo poking around in my past") until pushed; **draws after intense dreams** and visions [02:13:40].
- *(S05)* Insists he knows nothing about love ("I've only read about love"); Bebo's Insight reads that "his very own body rejects that premise."
- *(S06, per the written recap)* **Doesn't really know how the ice happens**, but it "always seems to occur at moments of heightened emotion."
- *(S06)* **Seeks water to calm down** (fled the tavern for a pond; cf. S07 "look for the water").
- *(S06)* **Apologizes for the harm his magic does:** went to find Aiph to apologize for freezing the tavern's patrons.
