# Isla "Bruin" Kaplan

> **POV character.** Her in-character journal lives at [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan Journal]].

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* Wears a **bear pelt as a hood** — ears at the crown, fangs over the brow — which she lowers to reveal her face.
- *(S01)* An **intricate braid** beneath the pelt.
- *(S01)* Her eyes **flash bear-orange/yellow**.
- *(S01)* **Silent-footed / light on her feet** — moves unheard when she wants to.
- *(S03)* **Scarred knuckles** — from pouring alcohol on her wounds.

## Backstory
- *(S01)* Home is **"the Liriope Valley"** (her words) — cf. the [[Liriope River Net]] region. *(Possible colloquialism vs. STT — flagged; see session note.)*
- *(S01)* Came to [[Rhusatatiam]] because **"the water doesn't run like it used to,"** chasing a scent on the air that signals the world changing.
- *(S02/OOC, pre-campaign)* Origin concept confirmed: outside a major city, near the [[Liriope River Net]] gulf area — [[Mouth that Feeds]] (capital) as likely home base. Shifter physiology reacting to [[The Overgrowth]] before anyone has named it is her inciting motivation to join the [[Uprooters]]. Adam endorsed this concept and noted it would inform world-building decisions at his next session with Alec.
- *(S03)* Her lifelong **"scent"** is confirmed by [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]] to be **the world "shifting"** — something *becoming* something else — the same thing she's smelled her whole life without a word for it; it's why the water stopped running right, and why she left.
- *(S03)* Aiph reveals Bruin was **chosen partly for the violence to come.**
- *(OOC, player-stated 10/10/26)* Her **father and brother have gone missing**. Neither is confirmed dead, but both are **presumed dead**.
- *(OOC, player-stated 10/10/26)* The man she **trained with** (her trainer) **became her family**. He is among "the people who mean a lot to my life" from S03. *(Whether he is also missing: [Unknown/Ambiguous], player to confirm.)*
- *(S07)* Back in [[Rhusatatiam]], the overgrown city **"doesn't feel right"** to her: she knows how a forest feels and how a garden feels, and this is neither (Nature check, called by Alec). *Not asserted as linked to her scent or to [[The Overgrowth]].*

## Personality
- *(S01)* **Skittish, terse, keeps to the edges**; waits for a cue before she sits.
- *(S01)* **Scent-attuned** — she perceives a change on the air/water others can't.
- *(S02/OOC, pre-campaign)* **Feels the world changing before others name it**; protective of that knowledge; possibly fears what it means; values exploration as the path to answers.
- *(S02/062826)* **Anxious about self-disclosure in group settings** — visibly hesitated before answering Aiph's "why are you here" question; needs a clear cue or reassurance to open up.
- *(S02/062826)* **Physically examines unfamiliar beings** — picked up Bebo unsolicited to understand what it was; set it back down gently in exactly the same spot.
- *(S02/062826)* **Reads emotional states from small physical cues** — recognized Eliza's "looking for something" expression; registered the weight of silence around a closed door upstairs.
- *(S03)* **Eats aggressively but precisely — nothing wasted** (meat on the bone, fast and clean); the vegetable-eaters found it hard to watch.
- *(S03)* **Retires early** — "made for the morning"; an athlete who guards her rest.
- *(S03)* **Ethos: "assume trust until proven otherwise."** Trust is the default; you take it back only when someone earns you taking it back.
- *(S03)* **Brutally honest about loyalty** — told the party she'd "sell all of you for people who mean a lot to my life," and that this doesn't mean they can't work together; she wants everyone to know exactly where she stands.
- *(S03)* **Prefers the direct path** — jumped into the dungeon rather than climb the ladder.
- *(S07)* **Gets "cooped up indoors"** and leaves for a natural spot or tree ("the same spot she was at the last time we were here").
- *(S07)* **Keeps her distance from [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]]** — stood and left soon after Mags arrived, consistent with her S03 resolution.
- *(S07)* **Took the whole bottle of wine** with her when she left the table.
- *(S07)* **Reads concealment plainly:** "As far as Bruin's concerned, we're lying."

## Inner Life & Turning Point (S07/092026)
Back at the Common Fire where it all started, Bruin hears Aiph's favor ("don't tell them") and reads it differently from the rest of the table: not a rivalry between recruiters but **embarrassment** — "something got in that she wasn't supposed to let in." Her answer is the simplest one available: "So just don't say anything." But keeping quiet and being comfortable with it are not the same thing. Once the other group arrives, Mags is at the bar, and the table turns strange around the half-kept secret, she decides that as far as she's concerned **they're lying**, and she leaves. On her way out she tries to catch Aiph's eye; Aiph clocks her and **looks away fast**. Bruin takes the whole bottle of wine outside to a growing place like last time, and the city itself turns on her: **this overgrowth isn't forest and isn't garden, and it "doesn't feel right."** Her turning point is quiet. She keeps both of her commitments (distance from Mags, silence for Aiph) while sitting alone with a felt wrongness she can't make right. She ends the session outside, apart from both the table and the bar, as the session closes on violence at the bar she has deliberately walked away from.

## Inner Life & Turning Point (S03/071226)
On the green train, Bruin finally names the thing she has carried her whole life. Her private scene with [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]] gives her lifelong "scent" a shape — the world *shifting*, *becoming* — and a purpose: Aiph chose her partly for the violence coming. Naming it is "a relief, like the ache after you finally set a bone." Rather than take the reassurance whole, Bruin negotiates a **self-protective boundary re [[Margaret "Mags" HoneyThatcher|Mags]]** (the "dragon lady" who is shifting too and hates change): she'll keep her distance, and only asks that Aiph *try* to keep Mags's fear off her. At the inn she stakes out her position on trust with total candor — she'd sell them all for the people who matter to her, and still work beside them — then **abstains from the party's hands-in gesture while explicitly affirming the trust** ("I will trust you guys, I just don't want to do that"). Her buy-in is verbal, not ritual: the same self-containment as lowering the bear pelt on her own terms. She closes the session by **jumping** into the dark rather than climbing down — the most Bruin possible way to enter a test.

## Inner Life & Turning Point (S02/062826)
Bruin entered Group K orientation braced for another moment like the interview — where honesty cost her something. Aiph pre-empted the hesitation ("you've already passed the test"), and for the second time in as many sessions, Bruin answered honestly because someone made her feel it was safe to. The choice to give Bebo her full weight, to let Bebo's eyes go glad, is her S02 equivalent of lowering the bear pelt: she lets the world in again. She ends the session boarding a train toward something she still cannot name, surrounded by people she has already begun to read.

## Inner Life & Turning Point (S01)
Bruin came to the [[Hearthread Hall]] meaning to be "a draft under a door" — invisible at the margins — but the wrongness in the air she'd chased across the continent was present *in the room itself*. Her turning point is the choice to **be honest** when [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] asked how far home was: she names the scent and the failing water aloud. When Mags reveals she herself **carries that same scent**, Bruin lowers the bear pelt and answers, *"I know, I can smell it from here"* — stepping, knowingly, off the edge of the recruiters' rehearsed plan. She ends the night uncertain what she is to these people, but chosen *because* of the scent. *(Mechanically, she read the recruiters poorly — see roll log — rendered in-character as "missing" the charged look because she had walked back inside.)*
