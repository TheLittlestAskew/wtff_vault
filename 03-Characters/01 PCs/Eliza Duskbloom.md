# Eliza Duskbloom

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* A **very young elf**; **curly brown hair** braided to one side.
- *(S01)* **Gray/tan vine-covered robes**; covered in **trinkets, necklaces, and bracelets**.

## Backstory
- *(S01)* **Forest-raised**, unused to people. Senses the world changing and wants answers.
- *(S01)* **Both recruiters uncannily feel they “know” her** despite never having met; [[Margaret “Mags” HoneyThatcher|Mags]] pre-selected her by a silent look.
- *(S02)* [[Aiphrumfite 'Aiph'|Aiph]] said Eliza “looks so familiar” during the Group K orientation. Eliza denied knowing her (Persuasion/Deception: 19 — Aiph narrowed eyes but let it go). The nature of their connection remains [Unknown/Ambiguous].

## Personality
- *(S01)* **Extremely timid**; twists her fingers when unsure.
- *(S02)* **Quiet and watchful** during large group interactions; something crossed her face when Aiph asked why she came — Bruin read it as “someone who came looking and is not ready to say looking for what.”
- *(S02)* **Protective instinct** — cast Healing Word on Bebo unprompted after Bebo took cold damage from Tobias.
- *(S03)* **Harvests/hoards food for the group** — gathered carrots in the conservatory car; remains timid and watchful in group settings.
- *(S04)* **Cautious:** found the tree orb but was "not brave enough to actually touch it."
- *(S04)* **Wry:** watching the party follow Bebo down the hole, "these people are insane."
- *(S04)* **Panics when her Ray of Frost splashes [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]]** and switches to healing her.
- *(S04)* **Reads Druidic** (one of two in the party, with Bruin).
- *(S05)* **Horrified at the harvesting** of the dragon [01:01:25].
- *(S05)* **Writes nonstop in her journal when shaken** (after the vision) [02:36:25].
