# BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)

> **Name discrepancy (S01):** Bebo states the acronym **"Botanical *Engineering* Balance Operator"** in play; the roster reads **"Botanical *Energy* Balance Operator."** Both preserved — DM/player to settle.

## Description / Appearance
- *(S01)* A **compact, self-fashioned construct**; **obsidian-black & bark-brown shell plates** with vivid **kintsugi repair seams** (blue/green/red/purple/amber). "Shaped through repeated repair."
- *(S01)* Moves on **three legs**.
- *(S02)* Has a **Heliolite core** embedded in the chest — pulsing dull white light, the same color as Bebo's eyes; confirmed as a canonical Artemesia term by Adam. Core is failing; Bebo needs to find more Heliolite or a suitable substitute.
- *(S02)* A **communication stone** is now embedded beside the Heliolite core — placed via Mending. The stone sank in, rock nubs snapped around the edge; half-submerged and glowing.

## Backstory
- *(S01)* Self-built/self-repaired over time; the kintsugi seams are the record of that.
- *(S07)* **"Bebo is from Bloom"** ([[The Bloom]]), as is [[Nyx]] (WtFR, crossover).

## Personality
- *(S01)* **Earnest, literal**; finds peace in the act of dancing ("the goal was not to dance well, the goal was to dance").
- *(S01)* Wants **"to learn… and to survive."**
- *(S02)* Uses **"people" as consistent third-person self-reference** ("People would like to hold the hand for longer").
- *(S02)* **Distributes snails as social gifts**; wandered the joint tavern with an escargot bowl on his head; placed snails on his own body and called them "friends."
- *(S02)* **Went completely still when [[Isla 'Bruin' Kaplan|Bruin]] picked him up** — described as "like it was trusting her with the whole weight of itself." Very heavy. This is how Bebo expresses trust.
- *(S02)* Shook Bruin's hand after; eyes went to happy-slits.
- *(S03)* **Coins "provisional trust"** — the party's operating vocabulary; put its arm in the center of the table to open the pact.
- *(S03)* **Studies the party to learn their tells** — over dinner (Insight nat 20/23), gaining an edge on reading any of the other five later.
- *(S03)* Continues **"people"/third-person self-reference.**
- *(S03)* **Tinkerer's curiosity** — wants to reverse-engineer [[Derek]]'s clap-activated device.
- *(S03)* **Well-read on the world** — History nat 20 on Sunroot Crossing (community-funded rail stop; Uprooters a lucrative, prestigious job).
- *(S07)* **"Bebo believes in truth"; "Bebo does not lie to friends"** — objects to Aiph's request ("Omission of truth is still not truth") and complies only under protest.
- *(S07)* **Refused to name its snail** ("Too easy to die... Unnecessary heartbreak") until [[Nyx]] named it [[Bloom]].
- *(S07)* **Uses used cooking grease on its joints** (ordered from the dwarven waiter; greased its previously frozen joints).
- *(S07)* **Crafts:** knitted a bag of holding; building **goggles of night** from the dungeon centipede's eyeball.
- *(S07)* **Deep bond with Nyx:** "Bebo cherishes our bond."

## Bebo Battery Procedure
[[Nyx]] transferred caretaking responsibilities for Bebo’s battery changes to [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] (primary) and [[Isla ‘Bruin’ Kaplan|Bruin]] (physical muscle) in [[Session 02 — Something’s Changing]]. Nyx gave Zarna her caretaker notebook with written instructions. The spare Heliolite supply is described as "not very large."

## Relationships
- **↔ [[Nyx]]:** tried (poorly) to entice Nyx onto the dance floor during the mingling (S01); strong attachment forged. Nyx transferred caretaking to Zarna + Bruin (S02); farewell at train platform — Bebo tapped embedded stone: "Bebo will miss you."
- **↔ [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]]:** Artie watched his dancing, "fascinated, or… scared."
- **↔ [[Aiphrumfite ‘Aiph’|Aiph]]:** Aiph dubbed him "animatronic nightmare"; selected into Group K.
- **↔ [[Isla ‘Bruin’ Kaplan|Bruin]]:** *(S02)* Went completely still when Bruin picked him up (trust). Shook hands; eyes to happy-slits. Bruin is now Bebo’s physical caretaker.
- **↔ [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]]:** *(S02)* Primary caretaker (battery procedures); received the caretaker notebook from Nyx.
- **↔ [[Tobias Wolfe|Tobias]]:** *(S02)* Voluntarily held Tobias’s hand beyond first Con save; took 3 cold damage; ice formed on limb. Tobias let go. Bebo accepted Tobias’s non-liability statement. *(S07)* Accepted Tobias's renewed apology for freezing it on its own terms ("Bebo believes people should face truth"); later called him "an odd duck."
- **↔ [[Nyx]] (S07, crossover):** Sending-stone messages both ways ("Bebo is asked to withhold truth. Bebo does not like this."); Nyx named its snail [[Bloom]]; Bebo whispered it had been told to lie and "does not lie to friends"; Nyx offered to "guard your truth." Bebo **showed Nyx dungeon spoils** (a scale, the eyeball, a vial of blood).
- **↔ [[Bloom]] (S07):** its snail companion, now named.
- **↔ [[Zarna Morganach|Zarna]] (S07):** Zarna watched Bebo craft so intently she stopped noticing anything else.
