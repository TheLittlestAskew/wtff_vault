## Description / Appearance
A half-orc, notably **small for her kind** — and she uses it deliberately. Soft eyes, lowered shoulders, a feigned innocence: she plays the underestimated, "helpless" role to bait the cruel, then unloads the fury they never saw coming. ## Backstory
Born beneath the towering trees of the [[Thornshade Forest]], in a moss-roofed wooden cottage with smoke forever curling from its stone chimney. Twin sister of [[Varro]]; the two were raised by their father [[Harkon Bloodroot]], a quiet, steady man who bore his grief with stoic resolve. Their mother, [[Graza]], a fierce half-orc hunter, was devoured by a forest-dwelling monstrosity when the twins were barely old enough to walk.

Despite that shadow, her childhood in the small, tightly-knit village of [[Mossmere]] was generally happy — friends, scraped knees, warm fires. Vega was always the bold one: climbing trees, leading games, testing limits. When she came of age she joined the [[Mossmere]] militia, not for pride but to protect: the children, the hearths, the land where her mother fell. In battle she fought like a storm, wild and unrelenting; the village elders said her love for her people was what fueled her strength.

**The [[Siege of Hollowbarrow]].** A horde of aberrant creatures spilled from the rotted heart of the forest. Vega fought day and night beside [[Varro]] and the militia. They won, but the cost was great. She survived; the images did not leave her. Eyes like black pits, screams, the way her axe sank too easily into flesh, the smell of burning blood.

In the aftermath, blame circled. During a joint effort with an adventuring party, Vega made a call she believed would save more lives — it cost the life of a party member. The party's leader, [[Kael Virethorn]], named her an enemy for it and still hunts her, convinced she is reckless and dangerous.

Shortly after, an estranged great-uncle from a distant clan died and bequeathed her a **greataxe**: worn but enchanted, it felt as though it had always belonged in her hands.

And then, in a glade where moonlight pooled like water, she met a **demigod** — a being of radiant presence and unknowable intent. It tested her in spirit, not in combat. She emerged changed, and never speaks of what passed between them.

## Personality
- **Small but mighty;** weaponizes being underestimated.
- Dry sense of humor; a deep love for underdogs and outcasts.
- **Alignment:** True Neutral. **Lifestyle:** modest (a small house).
- Her battle "rage" reads as devotion, not madness.
- **Bonds:** her bond to [[Mossmere]] and to her twin [[Varro]] is unshakable; the greataxe is her legacy, connecting her to her ancestors and a destiny beyond herself.
- **Flaws:** she carries guilt from the siege, and the nightmares make her question her own judgment; her pride can provoke her into conflict when she's underestimated.
