# The Hollow King

## Description / Appearance
A figure of legend, not present in play. Named in [[Alec]]'s opening monologue as the hero whose grief broke the Old World. Physical description: [Unknown/Ambiguous].

## Backstory
In the Old World's age of singular greatness — the **First Blooming** — the Hollow King was one grieving hero who **turned their power against death itself**. This ignited the **Ashfall Wars**, which scarred the continent and created **[[The Burn]]**. In the aftermath, survivors dismantled the cult of the hero and built a society of **balance** (the regions, [[The Flower Court]] and the **Pistil**, and the [[The Petal Line]]).
