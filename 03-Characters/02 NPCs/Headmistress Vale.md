## Backstory
- *(S05)* Ran the **orphanage** that took in Tobias after a priest found him as a baby, "left on a fountain in the middle of the harshest winter on record" [02:46:23]. *Identified with Vale by inference: S05 never says her name, but both sessions describe Tobias's headmistress.*

- *(S04)* Her saying, as Tobias quotes it: you expect the devil "to come in the back with horns and a tail," but "he usually arrives at the front door in a suit and a smile" [01:41:03].
