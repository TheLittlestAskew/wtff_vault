# The Pistil

## Description / Appearance
The **Pistil** is the axis at the center of **[[The Flower Court]]** — "not a ruler in the traditional sense, but the axis upon which the Court turns." The Pistil does not command; the Pistil **binds**. Not present in [[Session 01 — Winds in the East, Mist comin' in...]]; referenced in the opening monologue. *(Per existing [[The Flower Court]] lore, the seated Pistil is named **Serathiel Virellum**; the session referenced the role/office, not the individual by name — treat the name as established lore, not S01 canon.)*

## Backstory
The office of the Pistil was created in the founding of balance after the Ashfall Wars, holding the five regional Petals in consensus.
