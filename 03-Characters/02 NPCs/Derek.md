## Description / Appearance
- *(S03)* A **manic tinkerer / artificer-type** working at a bench in the train's staff & storage car. Not intoxicated — [[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]] (Perception 22) reads him as high on the act of creation ("his brain is a beehive").

## Backstory
- *(S03)* Works for an **architectural agency in [[The Bloom]]**; travels as staff aboard [[The Verdant Blossom]].

## Personality
- *(S03)* **Manic, eccentric, exuberant** about his inventions; wants everything to be able to "blow up just in case."
- *(S06)* **Overjoyed** that Artie was able to use the explosive he gave him; generous with more (gifted three party members).

## Abilities & Class Features
- *(S03)* Builds arcane gadgets. His **clap-activated light / mini-bomb** device registers as **evocation magic** ([[Artie (Art) Veyr|Artie]], Arcana 16).
