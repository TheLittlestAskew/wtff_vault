# Bloom

> ⚠️ **First-seen name (≤60%), S07.** "Bloom" is the name [[Nyx]] (WtFR, crossover) gave Bebo's snail at the table; spelling/canonical status unconfirmed. The snail itself predates S07 (riding Bebo's arm "since before the dungeon"); the sessions where it was first picked up (S04–S06) have no vault notes yet, so its origin is [Unknown/Ambiguous].

## Description / Appearance
- *(S07)* A **snail** that rides on [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]]'s forearm; found crawling up its forearm at the start of the session.

## Backstory
- *(S07)* Origin [Unknown/Ambiguous]: with Bebo since before the S07 session (sessions S04–S06 not yet written).
- *(S07)* **Named "Bloom" by [[Nyx]]**, because both she and Bebo are "from Bloom" ([[The Bloom]]): "You're from Bloom. I'm from Bloom. Why don't we call him Bloom?" Bebo had refused to name it until then.

## Personality
