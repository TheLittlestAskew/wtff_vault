# Bloom

> ⚠️ **First-seen name (≤60%), S07.** "Bloom" is the name [[Nyx]] (WtFR, crossover) gave Bebo's snail at the table; spelling/canonical status unconfirmed.
> **Origin (inferred, ~85%):** one of the escargot snails from the joint tavern feast in [[Session 02 — It Follows Me, So I Followed It|S02]]. In S07 Bebo says the snail "is from before we left. He was on the salad, remember?" [00:58:41]; in S02 the escargot bowl sat beside the mushroom and salad platters and Bebo wandered the tavern with snails crawling on it. The S04–S06 notes never mention it, so its time in the dungeon and through Bebo's freezing comes only from S07 table talk.

## Description / Appearance
- *(S07)* A **snail** that rides on [[BE-BO (Botanical Energy Balance Operator)|Bebo]]'s forearm; found crawling up its forearm at the start of the session.

## Backstory
- *(S02, inferred)* Came from the live escargot bowl at the joint tavern feast in [[Session 02 — It Follows Me, So I Followed It|S02]], which Bebo wore on its head while handing out snails as "friends." Bebo in S07: "Is from before we left. He was on the salad, remember?" [00:58:41]
- *(S04–S06, per S07)* Rode along through Aiph's test dungeon ("as much a dungeon diver as" Bebo [00:58:51]) and stayed on Bebo while it was frozen. At the table Zarna's player joked "Snail is now canonically resistant to cold" [00:15:36].
- *(S07)* **Named "Bloom" by [[Nyx]]**, because both she and Bebo are "from Bloom" ([[The Bloom]]): "You're from Bloom. I'm from Bloom. Why don't we call him Bloom?" Bebo had refused to name it until then.
- *(S08)* **Released.** Bebo: the snail "will not be able to thrive" in [[The Burn]]. After riding under Bebo's top hat to the Festivus of Warmth, it was left with [[Nyx]] present on "the greenest, most vegetative leaf" of a large, healthy tree with a wooden ledge in the main greenhouse [01:55:15]–[01:57:29].

## Personality
- *(S07)* Scooted up to the bowl of used cooking grease when Bebo offered it some.
